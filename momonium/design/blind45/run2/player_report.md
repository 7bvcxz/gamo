The ten minutes are up. This is my report as a first-time player.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00** The game opened on a small floating island with a white bunny (Momo), already on "Day 2". A prompt said "Tap an empty tile." I tapped it and built a Farm for $10, which was all my money.
- **00:20** It turned night almost at once. I worked out that a day lasts only 30 seconds. The farm took 4 seconds to build.
- **00:32–01:05** I sowed and watered by tapping the farm, with each step filling a progress bar. On one tap Momo was standing on the farm, so I selected Momo instead of the farm.
- **01:11–02:30** The tutorial said "Tap a Momo", and this was where I got stuck. Momo kept walking, so my taps landed on the sea, which opened a $35 land plot ("Not enough money"), or on the farm. Twice I selected Momo and tapped the pencil, but Momo had moved and the tutorial went back to "Tap a Momo". In the meantime the tomatoes ripened and I harvested them. My third try at the pencil worked.
- **02:36** Edit mode, "Tap tiles: 0/4". I added the farm to Momo's route. A big tips box then gave four rules at once: watering cooldown, building, demolishing, and "half comes back".
- **03:09–04:05** I looked through the menus:
  - Mailbox: empty.
  - Shop: "Hire Momo $25".
  - Research: a large tech tree that needs flasks I don't have.
  - Status: becoming a City needs Land 4/9, Momo 1/3, Research 0/3 and Value $5/$1.5K.
- **04:25–05:10** Momo farmed by itself and my XP went up, but coins stayed at 5 for a while. Then Momo sent a letter proposing "widen the field", and I added it to the research list.
- **05:21–06:40** I built a second farm. I could sow it myself with the Sow button, then added it to Momo's route as tile 2. Momo reached "Farm skill 1" and sent a second letter, "water less often".
- **07:13–07:36** With Momo selected, tapping a farm seemed to send Momo there instead of selecting the farm, and I couldn't find a way to deselect Momo.
- **08:12** I reached Lv 2, but it was so quiet I almost missed it. The Research lab now costs $45 and the Lumberyard $80. Under Special I found landmarks: a School at Lv 4, a Clock Tower at Lv 5 and a Momo Monument at Lv 6.
- **08:57–09:31** I built and sowed a third farm. I ended with 3 of 4 tiles used, $5 and Lv 2.

**2. What was fun**
- The art is lovely, and the fast day/night cycle with its sunsets and moon is pretty.
- Momo is charming. The letters, where Momo suggests the upgrades, were my favourite part: research felt like Momo's idea, not a menu.
- Setting Momo's route with numbered tiles was clear and satisfying once it worked.
- Watching three plots grow at different stages was relaxing.
- The Status page gave me a clear long-term goal (becoming a City), and the landmarks gave me something to look forward to.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- "Tap a Momo" with a moving target was the worst moment. Taps landed late, so I hit the sea or the farm, and the tutorial looped. I lost about a minute and a half there.
- The pencil moves with Momo, so it was hard to hit. "Its tiles light up. The pencil edits them" didn't make sense at first, because nothing had lit up yet.
- When Momo stands on a farm, I can't select the farm. With Momo selected, every tap seems to move Momo, and I never found how to deselect.
- I don't understand when a harvest turns into money. Coins came in late and only sometimes ($5 now and then), while XP went up at once. A cut-off "1 c…" kept showing at the right edge under the XP bar, possibly a crop count that is hidden.
- Money was very slow: $5 per harvest, against a $45 lab and a $1.5K City goal.
- The level-up had almost no fanfare.
- Research needs flasks, and nothing told me where they come from. I assume the Research lab.
- The tips box gave me too much text at once.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Probably yes, for a while. It's cozy, Momo's letters give it personality, and I want the Research lab and a second Momo. But the slow, unclear money flow and the fiddly tapping on a moving Momo would make me put it down soon if they don't improve.

**5. Confirmation**
I used only the play.mjs commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed. I didn't open, list or search any other files, code, docs or websites.
