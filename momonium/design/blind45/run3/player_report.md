I played the full ten minutes and finished at Day 21, level 2 (44/180 EXP), with 5 coins, three farms on the 2×2 island and one Momo.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00** A small grassy island in the sea, a bunny-like creature seen from behind, and a hint: "Tap an empty tile". The HUD said Day 2, 11/30 sec, 10 coins, Lv1. Days last 30 seconds and the sky cycles through dawn, noon, sunset and moonlit night, which is lovely.
- **00:08–00:26** Tapping the tile opened a build menu: Farm $10, Mine (Lv3), Lumberyard (Lv2), Research lab (Lv2), Special. I built a farm in about 4 seconds and got 5 EXP.
- **00:33** The hint said "Tap the farm to sow", but the bunny had walked onto the farm, so I tapped her instead. That's how I learned her name is Momo, with "Free time" and an Assign button.
- **00:38–00:47** I worked out that you press Assign and then tap a building. She switched to "Working at Farm" with a skill level, a job count and "Route: Nearest first".
- **01:07** I found "the bar fills" from the hint: it's a green ring above the farm.
- **01:30–01:56** The pencil sets which tiles Momo looks after (up to 4). I added an empty tile without knowing whether that was useful. Then a long text box came up covering watering, building and demolishing all at once.
- **02:41** First harvest: EXP went from 9 to 29, but coins stayed at 0. A popup starting "1 c…" was clipped at the right edge of the screen, and I never got to read it.
- **02:50–03:06** Coins became 5. A letter from Momo arrived, "What if we widened the field?", with a "Let's research it" button.
- **03:14** The Research tree has tabs for Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games and more. Field expansion costs 3 flasks, $20 and a research lab. I never found out what a flask is or how to get one.
- **03:30–03:50** The Status screen shows a Village→City goal: 9 land, 3 Momos, 3 research levels, $1.5K produced. The Money tab has "Island worth $49 · Sell…", which looked scary, so I left it alone.
- **04:02** The Shop has a single item: Hire Momo for $25.
- **04:52** I worked out that crops are sold at dawn, not at harvest. Then I had 10 coins.
- **05:12–05:28** I built a second farm on the tile I'd already added to Momo's area, and she went over to it on her own. That felt great.
- **05:37** The farm's info panel showed "Cycle by hand 1:10" against "Cycle by Momo 1:48", so I can farm faster myself.
- **06:00–06:19** Twice more, Momo was standing on the farm I tried to tap, and I selected her instead.
- **06:46–07:04** A second letter, "Maybe we could water less often", turned into another research proposal.
- **07:14** I tapped a ripe farm and Momo stepped onto it at the same moment. My follow-up tap aimed at Harvest landed on her **Release** button, and she went back to "Free time".
- **07:27–07:48** I re-assigned her and reached level 2. Assigning again had reset her tiles, so I used the pencil to add farm 2 back.
- **08:01** Special buildings are landmarks: Clock Tower (Lv5), School (Lv4) and Momo Monument (Lv6).
- **08:20–09:24** I built a third farm and sowed and watered it myself. The Water button shows three dots for "water ×3". Momo never got farm 3 added to her tiles.

**2. What was fun**
- The art and the day/night cycle are beautiful, and Momo is adorable.
- Upgrades arrive as handwritten letters from Momo. That was the most charming part of the game.
- Momo walking to a new farm by herself was a real moment of satisfaction.
- Sowing and watering by hand made me feel like I was helping.

**3. What confused me or got me stuck**
- Momo stands on the tile I'm trying to tap, so I kept selecting her instead of the building. It cost me a mis-tap on Release.
- Coins don't arrive at harvest; they come at dawn, and nothing tells you that. For a long stretch I thought harvesting gave no money.
- A "1 c…" popup kept getting cut off at the right edge, under the level bar.
- Re-assigning Momo silently cleared her tile list.
- I never learned what flasks are or how to get them.
- Money comes very slowly: about $5 per crop per roughly 1.5 minutes, with a lab costing $45 and a lumberyard $80.
- The "pencil" tip came back after I'd pressed Got it, and one tip was a large block of text.
- I couldn't tell whether my Harvest tap did anything while Momo was harvesting the same farm.

**4. Would I keep playing?** Probably for a little while. It's cozy and the letters make me curious about what Momo will suggest next. But by minute 10 I was waiting on a slow trickle of 5-coin crops for a lab I couldn't afford. If income doesn't speed up soon, or something new doesn't happen, I'd drift away.

**5. Confirmation:** I used only the play.mjs commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshot files they printed.
