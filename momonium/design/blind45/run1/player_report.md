Playtest report (first-time player, about 10 minutes, ended on day 20 at level 2 with $15)

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00** A tiny floating island at night with a white bunny seen from behind, and the hint "Tap an empty tile". I tapped it, it turned to day 2, and a build menu opened. I could only afford the Farm ($10). Mine, Lumberyard and Research lab were locked by level.
- **00:17–00:28** I built the farm in about 4 seconds and was back to $0. Days last only 30 seconds, so day and night flip constantly. It's pretty, but I lost track of time.
- **00:34–01:02** I sowed and watered by hand. Each step is a 5-second bar, and watering needs three taps. There's a 10-second wait between waterings, which the button showed as "10s left". The sprouts visibly growing felt good.
- **01:08–01:32** The hint said "Tap a Momo", so I learned the bunny is a Momo. The next hint, "Its tiles light up. The pencil edits them", made no sense to me. I tried twice to tap the pencil, but Momo kept walking away and the pencil went with it, so I missed both times. The tutorial hint then vanished.
- **01:51–02:28** On a later try I got a clearer hint: "The pencil edits which tiles this Momo looks after". I painted the farm tile and Momo walked over and started farming by itself. This was the moment it clicked.
- **02:36–03:27** The Mailbox was empty. Research turned out to be a big tree (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games…) that costs flasks I didn't have. Under Status I found the goal "Village → City": Land 4/9, Momo 1/3, Research 0/3, $5 of $1.5K produced.
- **03:40** The Shop sells one thing, Hire Momo for $25.
- **04:04–04:39** The Special build slot holds landmarks (Clock Tower, School, Momo Monument). Momo sent me a letter, "What if we widened the field?", that suggests a research. I loved it.
- **04:50–05:18** My money sat at $5 while tomatoes kept growing. The Money tab explained it: crops sell when the day rolls over.
- **05:28–06:40** I built a second farm with $10 and added it to Momo's tiles. Then I switched Momo's route from "Nearest first" to "Row by row" and it said "Comparing: a few days are needed on each side".
- **06:50–07:23** A second letter suggested "water less often". The Research screen finally told me where flasks come from: "Build a research lab".
- **07:41** Momo's panel said "Since the change: 1.4 → 3 jobs a day".
- **08:23–08:36** I reached level 2 almost without noticing. The research lab costs $45 and the lumberyard $80.
- **08:49–09:22** At dawn I got $15 (5 crops made so far). The Life tab says my Momo sleeps outdoors and happiness is 0. The Stats tab is a wall of numbers, about +$2 a day.

**2. What was satisfying**
- The art is lovely: the island, the day/night sky and the Momo.
- Painting tiles and then watching Momo walk over and work by itself felt good.
- The crops visibly grow from seeds to sprouts to leaves to tomatoes.
- The letters from Momo, styled like airmail, are my favourite part. The upgrade ideas come from my worker, not from a menu.
- The panel tells me what a change did ("1.4 → 3 jobs a day") and the route comparison is clever.
- The goals are clear: City, landmarks, a research lab, and a home for Momo.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- "Its tiles light up. The pencil edits them" meant nothing at first. Because the pencil moves with a walking Momo, I missed it twice and lost the tutorial thread.
- Money comes in only at dawn, so for several days I thought my harvests earned nothing.
- A popup at the far right edge was cut off ("1 c…", "2 c…") and I never could read it.
- The 1 and 2 numbers on the route tiles kept swapping between the farms.
- I accepted "Watering efficiency" from a letter, but in Research it still looked locked and didn't seem to be queued.
- Reaching level 2 had no celebration, and I almost missed that new buildings had opened up.
- I couldn't tell whether the route change or the second farm caused the 1.4 → 3 jobs gain.
- "1 sleeps outdoors" made me want to build a house, but there's no house in the build menu.
- Income is slow: about $2 a day against a $45 lab, so the last few minutes were mostly waiting.
- The Stats tab is too much like a spreadsheet for a new player.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes, probably for a while. I'm attached to the Momo, I want to give it a home, hire a second one and open the research lab, and the letters make me curious what it will suggest next. The risk is the slow economy after the first few minutes. If nothing new unlocks soon, waiting through the 30-second days would wear me down.

**5. Confirmation**
I used only the play commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed. I opened no other files, did no searching and did not use the web.
