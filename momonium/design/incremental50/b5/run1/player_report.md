I turned the island into a City with about a second to spare. The banner said "Now a City · Special slots 1 → 2" and the corner goal moved on to "Next: Nation 0/4".

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:25:** I started on a tiny island with a bunny and a tomato bed. The prompts walked me through harvesting, sowing and watering, and I learned the bunny is a "Momo". A day lasts 30 seconds, so day and night came and went almost at once.
- **00:30–00:53:** I used the pencil to give the Momo the farm tile. A long tip covered the screen ("Any empty tile builds a farm, mine, lumberyard or lab… bin… check… half comes back"). After that the Momo farmed by itself and income went from +$4/min to +$20/min.
- **01:00–01:30:** A letter from Momo, "What if we widened the field?", suggested a research. The Research screen said I needed 3 flasks, $20 and a research lab. The lab was locked until level 2.
- **01:35–02:44:** I built a second farm for $10, which removed the tree and bush. I reached level 2, saw that sea tiles can be bought, and built the lab for $40. Flasks started to collect.
- **02:54–04:18:** I couldn't afford to hire at $100, read the other letters, and started Field expansion. I hired Mochi and put Mochi on the lab.
- **04:21:** Tapping "Next: City 0/4" opened Status with a clear checklist: Land 4/9, Momo 2/3, Research levels 1/3, Value $248/$1.5K. From here I knew what I was doing.
- **04:45–06:40:** I bought land for $65, researched Field expansion level 2 and then Bigger harvests, and hired Pomu for $160, which made the Momo goal 3/3. I bought more land for $85.
- **06:49–07:31:** At level 4, the Mine needed $120 plus 10 wood, so I built a Lumberyard for $80 and a wood counter appeared.
- **07:40–09:07:** I bought land for $110, $145 and $190, dragging the camera to reach the last tiles. Research went to Watering efficiency. The goal went to City 3/4.
- **09:15–09:59:** I built a Mine with the wood I had saved, the goal reached City 4/4, and I pressed "Become a City!" just in time. Income was +$342/min by then.

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The art is lovely: the Momos, the lab, the mine, and the sky moving from sunrise to night.
- Watching the Momos work on their own while income climbed from $4 to $342 per minute felt good.
- Getting research ideas as letters from the Momos was charming.
- The Status checklist gave me a clear goal, and each "Next: City x/4" step felt rewarding.
- Each piece of land I bought visibly grew the island.
- Saving wood until I could finally build the Mine was a nice small chain to work out.

**3. What confused me or got in the way**
- Tutorial bubbles sat on top of the panel I was meant to use: the Momo panel, and the "1/4 tiles" counter.
- The early tip about building and demolishing was too much text at once.
- One "Got it" tap also selected a sea tile behind it, leaving a "$65 Tap again to buy" tag on screen.
- The $65 price tag overlapped the Experiment button.
- The Momo panel and building action bars kept covering the bottom of the island, including the next tile to buy. I had to tap the sky to close them.
- When I opened the build menu, I couldn't tell which tile it would build on.
- I didn't know building a farm would remove the tree.
- "Let's research it" only adds the idea to a list ("On the research list"). It doesn't start anything.
- The flask count sometimes dropped by an odd amount when I started research.
- The Research card in Status said "Lv 3" while the goal said "Research levels 2/3".
- "Researched: Field expansion…" appeared as small grey text over the sky and was easy to miss.
- The Mailbox red dot stayed on for a long time.
- A Momo with a letter bubble kept following it around, and I never learned what tapping it does.
- I didn't know whether the lumberyard needed its own Momo; the workers seemed to wander between buildings.
- I never opened the Store, Money, Records, Life or Stats tabs, the "Games" research tab, or "Special 0/1".

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes, for a while. The pace was fast, my goals were always clear once I found Status, and I want to see the City's second special slot and what "Nation" needs. I'd likely stop if the costs get steep and the screen keeps getting cluttered by overlapping panels.

**5. Confirmation**
I used only the play commands (look, tap, drag, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshot files they printed. I didn't open, list or search any other files and didn't look anything up.
