Playtest report (Momonium v0.50.0, run b5/run2)

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:30.** It opens on a small floating island with a vegetable patch and a white bunny. One step at a time the game told me to tap the ripe crop, tap to sow, then tap again to water. I got coins (10→14) and XP. A day lasts only 30 seconds, so day and night kept flipping, which was pretty.
- **00:25–00:55.** "Tap a Momo" told me the bunny is a Momo, a worker. With the pencil I gave it the farm tile, and it started farming by itself (+$12/min).
- **01:00–01:20.** A mail bubble appeared. It was a letter from Momo, "What if we widened the field?", written on airmail paper, with a "Let's research it" button. I opened Research and found a large tree (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games…). Field expansion needed 3 flasks and $20.
- **01:30–02:20.** "Build a research lab" opened the build menu, but the lab only unlocks at Lv2. I built a second farm instead. The game picked the tile for me and my nice tree disappeared, which was a little sad. I reached Lv2 and got a hint about buying the sea next to the island ($65). I built the lab ($40).
- **02:30–03:30.** My one Momo kept walking back and forth between farm and lab, so crops sat ripe. I hired Mochi ($100) and put Mochi on the lab, then hit Lv3.
- **03:50–05:00.** I researched Field expansion, which finished fast with a nice toast. More letters from Momo (water less often, give the soil something back) led me straight to the next research items. I started Bigger harvests. The Games tab offers "Software: an island game studio" at Lv6, which made me smile.
- **05:20–06:00.** "Special" turned out to be landmarks (Clock Tower, School, Momo Monument), all locked. The Mine needed $120 plus 10 wood, so I built a Lumberyard ($80) and hired Pomu ($160). "Next: City" went to 1/4.
- **06:10–06:35.** I tried to assign Pomu. The lumberyard was "Another Momo works here", and tapping the lab selected Mochi instead of assigning Pomu. I lost track of who did what.
- **06:40–07:40.** I bought the sea plot and a new grassy tile rose up. I reached Lv4. Sharp axes was locked behind "Lumber mastery Lv2". I started Watering efficiency and built the Mine.
- **07:50–08:40.** Mochi wrote a letter about a Tree nursery. I hired Mugi ($260, after waiting for coins) and assigned Mugi to the Mine.
- **08:50.** I finally tapped "Next: City" and found the checklist: Land 5/9, Momo 4/3 ✓, Research levels 2/3, Value produced $1.1K/$1.5K. After that the goal was clear.
- **09:00–09:47.** I reached City 2/4, bought another plot (my first tap on the "$85" tag did nothing), and built a farm on it. Mugi's first letter, "Green streaks in the rock", had just arrived when time ran out.

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The Momos' letters were my favourite part. Each worker writes about its own job and suggests the next research, so I always had a gentle next step and it felt personal.
- The art is lovely: the 30-second day/night sky, the lab cottage, the mine with its cart and lantern, land rising out of the sea.
- Progress was always visible: income going up each minute, toasts like "Researched: Field expansion", new counters (wood, stone, flasks) appearing as things unlocked, and a new named Momo with each hire.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- **Who works where.** One Momo kept leaving the farms for the lab. Tapping Experiment seemed to pull a worker away. Tapping a building in Assign mode selected the Momo standing there instead of assigning the new one. I never found out where Pomu ended up.
- **The game placed my second farm itself** and removed the tree. I didn't know which tile a build would go on until it started.
- **"Build a research lab" from the Research screen** opened a menu where the lab was locked.
- **The "Next: City" goal** sat in the corner for 9 minutes before I tapped it. Nothing showed me it was a checklist, and I first guessed it meant number of Momos.
- **"Lumber mastery Lv2"** in red: I didn't know how to raise mastery. I only saw later, on the Status screen, that each industry has its own level.
- **Small things:** tapping the "$85" tag didn't select the plot, and the Level-up toast faded too quickly to read.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. I had just learned the City checklist (land 9, value $1.5K), the mine had just started, and Mugi's copper hint was waiting. The letters made me want to see what each Momo would suggest next. I'd want clearer worker assignment before investing much more, though.

**5.** I used only the play commands (look, tap, drag, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed.
