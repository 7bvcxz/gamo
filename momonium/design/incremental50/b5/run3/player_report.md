The ten minutes are up. My report as the player:

## 1. Timeline

- **00:00** The first screen was a night-time floating island with a bunny (a "Momo") and a tomato bed, and a hint said "Tap the ripe crop". The top-left said "Day 2", which surprised me. I tapped the crop and got 10 → 14 coins and some XP.
- **00:14** The hint said "Tap the farm to sow". I tapped the bed, but Momo was standing on it, so I selected Momo by accident. At **00:22** I tapped the free edge of the plot instead, which sowed it, and tapping again watered it. A day lasts 30 seconds, so day and night flip every few taps. It looks lovely but felt a bit hectic.
- **00:36–01:02** The tutorial had me tap Momo, then the pencil, then the farm tile. Momo started farming by itself and coins went up with no input from me. That's when I understood it's an idle game.
- **01:10–01:24** The Mailbox had a letter from Momo, "What if we widened the field?", with a "Let's research it" button. Accepting it added Field expansion to the research list.
- **01:31–01:40** The Research tree has tabs (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games…). Field expansion needed 3 flasks and $20. I tapped "Build a research lab", but the lab only opens at Lv2, so I built a second farm for $10 instead. A tip said I can buy the sea next to the island for $65.
- **02:06–02:25** I reached Lv2 and built a research lab for $40. Tapping the lab gave an "Experiment" button that makes flasks one at a time.
- **03:31** I hired a second Momo, Mochi, for $100. My pencil tap on the lab cancelled edit mode instead of selecting the tile. After that I learned the flow: press **Assign**, then tap a building. The lab told me "Another Momo works here", so I put Mochi on the second farm.
- **04:52** I started Field expansion, and income roughly doubled. Two more letters unlocked Watering efficiency and Bigger harvests in the tree, so I worked out that letters are how research gets unlocked.
- **06:10** The "Next: City 0/4" button opened a checklist: Land 4/9, Momo 2/3, Research levels 1/3, Value $472/$1.5K. From then on I had a clear goal.
- **06:44–07:09** I bought a sea tile ($65, tap once to see the price and again to buy), reached Lv4 and hired a third Momo, Pomu, for $160. City went to 1/4.
- **07:24–07:47** A Mine needed $120 plus 10 Wood, with the wood shown in red, so I built a Lumberyard ($80) first. A Momo started working it on its own.
- **08:10–09:04** I started Watering efficiency, bought another land tile ($85), and accepted a Tree nursery proposal from Mochi.
- **09:20–10:00** I built the Mine and tried Dig. I ended with 566 coins, Lv4, about $242/min, 1 stone, 17 flasks, and City 2/4.

## 2. What felt satisfying or fun

- The workers write me letters, and those letters unlock research. It's charming, and it explains why each upgrade exists.
- It's satisfying to watch the island grow tile by tile and see income climb from +4/min to about +240/min.
- When something can't be bought yet, the missing requirement shows in red (for example "Wood 10" on the Mine), so I always knew what to do next.
- The City checklist turned a pile of options into one clear goal.
- The art is soft and cute. The day/night sky is pretty, and the heart bubbles over the bunnies made me smile.

## 3. What confused me or got me stuck

- I kept tapping the Momo when I meant the building or farm it was standing on. It happened on the first farm and again on the lab.
- In pencil mode, tapping the lab seemed to exit the mode instead of selecting the tile.
- The Assign and Release buttons move around between panel states, and one of my taps landed on the wrong one.
- I never really knew which bunny was which. Momo seemed to wander over to the lab without me sending it there.
- The sea-tile "$65" price label sat on top of the lab's Experiment button.
- 30-second days are so short that "Day 2 → Day 22" felt meaningless. I also didn't learn whether night matters for gameplay.
- I never opened the "Special 0/1" building slot or the Games tab, so I don't know what they are.

## 4. Would I keep playing?

Yes, for a while. There's always a next small goal (City checklist, next research, next tile), the letters make me curious about what the bunnies will suggest next, and watching income tick up is relaxing. My worry is that once the farms run themselves there isn't much for me to do except wait and tap "buy". I'd want the Mine, Smelting and City steps to add new decisions.
