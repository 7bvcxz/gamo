The ten minutes are up. Here's my report as a first-time player.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:32:** A cute floating island with a tomato patch and a bunny. The prompts were easy to follow: I tapped the ripe crop (+$4), sowed, watered, then tapped the bunny. A full day lasts only 30 seconds, so the sky cycles through dawn, noon, dusk and night fast, which looks lovely. At 00:12 my "tap the farm" landed on the bunny standing in the field and opened her panel instead; tapping the left edge of the field worked.
- **00:38–00:58:** The pencil let me pick the tiles Momo looks after. I gave her the farm, and from then on she farmed by herself.
- **01:04–01:17:** A hint said I could buy the sea next to the island. Tapping once shows the price and tapping again buys it. I bought land for $25 and the island grew.
- **01:27–02:00:** The mailbox had letters from Momo with ideas like "widen the field" and "water less often". "Let's research it" puts each idea on the research list. I loved this.
- **02:04–02:40:** The Research tree said "Build a research lab", so I tapped empty grass, got a build menu (Farm / Mine / Lumberyard / Research lab / Special) and built a lab for $40.
- **02:48:** My tap on the crop selected the bunny instead, for the second time.
- **03:18–04:06:** I hired a second bunny, Mochi, for $50. I pressed Assign and tapped the lab, then the farm, and she stayed on "Free time" both times with no reason given.
- **04:19–04:36:** I built a second farm and Mochi went to it on her own. I started Field expansion with 7/3 flasks and $20; it finished quickly and the fields grew visibly fuller.
- **05:25–06:05:** More letters, then I started Bigger harvests (2 min). The Games tab shows a "Software: an island game studio" research. That's a great long-term hook.
- **06:24–06:53:** I built a lumberyard ($80) and hired Pomu ($80, prices go up with each hire). Assign plus a tap on the building worked this time.
- **07:05:** Status showed a clear goal for Village → City: Land 5/9, Momo 3/3 done, Research levels 1/3, Value produced $412/$1.5K.
- **07:36–09:34:** I bought two more pieces of land ($35, $45), built a third farm and started Watering efficiency research. The Mine unlocked once the lumberyard had made 10 wood, so I built it ($120) and hired a fourth bunny, Mugi ($130), for it.
- **End:** Level 4, four bunnies, three farms, a lab, a lumberyard and a mine, earning about $274/min (up from about $4/min at the start).

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The art, the fast day-and-night sky and the bunnies are charming.
- The letters are the best part. Upgrades are pitched as the bunny's own ideas, so researching them feels personal.
- Progress is easy to see: fields fill up, the island grows, buildings appear and the $/min keeps climbing.
- The goal checklist in Status gave me direction at exactly the point I started wondering what to aim for.
- Each unlock leads to the next, like wood unlocking the mine.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- The bunny stands right on top of the field, so tapping the crop selected her instead (twice). It was annoying.
- Assigning Mochi failed silently, twice. My guess is each building takes one worker and Momo already held both the farm and the lab, but the game never said so. A short "this building is full" message would help.
- Bunnies sometimes walked to new buildings without me assigning them, so I was never sure who was doing what or whether assigning was even needed.
- Some labels were unclear: the "0/1" next to building names, the flask numbers (7/3 is more than needed, so I wasn't sure whether flasks get spent), "Special 0/1", and the speech bubbles over bunnies, some of which are mail and some hearts.
- I never opened Settings, or the Store, Money, Records, Life and Stats tabs in Status, so I don't know what's there.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. Ten minutes went by fast, the loop of earning, expanding, hiring and researching feels good, and I have open goals pulling me on: becoming a City, the next research levels and the game-studio research. The few clumsy taps and the silent assign failure are irritations, not reasons to quit.

**5. Confirmation**
I used only the play commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and opened only the screenshots they printed. I didn't open, list or search any other file, look at code or docs, or browse the web.
