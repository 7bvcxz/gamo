**Momonium, my first 10 minutes as a new player (v0.50.0)**

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:24, tutorial.** A cute floating island with a bunny in overalls and a tomato bed. Prompts told me to tap the ripe crop (+$4), tap the farm to sow, and tap again to water. A day only lasts 30 seconds, so the sun set while I was still in the tutorial. That was a pleasant surprise.
- **00:24–00:57, Momos and tiles.** "Tap a Momo" taught me the bunny is a Momo. I tapped the pencil, tapped the farm, and confirmed with the checkmark, which assigned it to the farm. A tip said empty tiles can hold a farm, mine, lumberyard or lab. Another said I could buy the sea next to the island for $25. Money started flowing on its own, about +$12/min.
- **01:03–01:41, letters.** The Mailbox had letters from Momo ("What if we widened the field?", "Maybe we could water less often"). Each had a "Let's research it" button. This is my favourite part of the game.
- **01:48–02:59, first lab and research.** The Research screen is a big tree with tabs (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games…). Field expansion needed 3 flasks, $20 and a lab. I built a Research lab for $40 on the left tile. The Momo split its time between lab and farm, and at 02:59 I started Field expansion.
- **03:19–03:48, second Momo.** The Shop only offers "Hire Momo" ($50). I hired Mochi and assigned it to the lab.
- **04:05–04:38, Lv3.** Another letter arrived ("give the soil something back"). Field expansion Lv2 cost 4 flasks and $36.
- **04:48–05:47, wood.** The Mine needed $120 plus 10 wood, so I built a Lumberyard ($80) first. I hired Pomu ($80) and assigned it there. Each Momo has its own name, which I liked.
- **06:06, research.** Started Bigger harvests ($60, 2 min).
- **06:47–07:04, Mine and the goal.** I built the Mine. Then I opened Status and finally saw a goal: Village → City needs Land 4/9, Momo 3/3, Research levels 2/3 and Value produced $530 of $1.5K. From then on I knew what I was playing for.
- **07:21–07:50, more land.** Bought sea tiles for $25 and $35; each new tile arrived with bushes. Built a second farm.
- **08:07–08:51, Lv4.** Hired Mugi ($130) for the mine and started Greenhouse ($120). Status showed Momo and Research complete, Land 6/9 and Value $986 of $1.5K.
- **09:01–09:55, hunting for land.** Bought a tile for $45. After that, the next sea plot was off-screen and I couldn't see where it was. Dragging did nothing while a Momo panel was open, but worked once it closed. Even then no dashed outline was visible. I tapped blindly into the sea, found a $55 plot, and bought it with the clock running out (Land should now be 8/9). I finished with $486 at +$370/min.

**2. What was satisfying or fun**
- The art, and the 30-second day/night cycle. The island looks different every time I glance at it.
- The Momos writing letters that turn into research. It feels like they have ideas and I'm helping them, not just buying upgrades.
- Steady growth: +$4/min to +$370/min, four named Momos, and new land that sprouts bushes.
- The City checklist in Status made everything click.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- The City goal is hidden in Status, and I found it 7 minutes in. Until then I was poking around with no direction.
- Even when assigned to a building, Momos wander to other buildings, and the panel says things like "Mochi: On its rounds — the next crop is ripening". I couldn't tell what an assignment actually changes.
- "Research lab · 0/1", "4 tiles" and "Special 0/1" were never explained.
- Once the island grew past the screen, I couldn't find the next buyable sea tile. The camera wouldn't drag while a panel was open, and the next plot's outline only appeared after I tapped empty sea.
- Late in the run money piled up ($400+) with nothing obvious to spend it on.
- Tutorial bubbles overlapped the Momo panel, and I never found out what the Mine produces.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. It's cozy and relaxing, the letters are charming, and the City checklist gives a clear next goal (one more tile and about $500 of value). I'd want the goal shown earlier, and buyable land easier to find.

**5. Confirmation**
I only used the play commands (look, tap, drag, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed. I did not open, list or search any other file, code, documentation or website.
