**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The art and the bunnies are charming, and the day/night cycle is beautiful.
- Buying sea tiles and watching the island grow is the best moment in the game.
- Letters from Momo that become research ideas are a sweet way to deliver upgrades.
- Upgrades pay off visibly: crops worth $4, then $8, and floating "+$" numbers.
- The City checklist gave me a clear and motivating goal.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- **Hiring Momos was hard to find.** It's hidden behind a generic "Shop" button that I took for an in-app purchase store. The City checklist asks for 3 Momos but nothing points you to where to get them.
- **One Momo juggling the farm and the lab** made income drop with no explanation.
- **Taps go to the wrong thing:** the mailbox list shifting under my finger, and the Momo panel covering the island so my tap hit Assign.
- **Some hints were long walls of text,** or overlapped the panel they were describing.
- **Unclear labels:** I couldn't tell what "Research lab 0/1" meant at the bottom of the screen, or why it switched between 0/1 and 1/1. The "..." bubbles over Momos were also a mystery.
- **The island grew past the right edge of the screen,** and I didn't find a way to pan or zoom.
- **Tapping near the island to buy land was fiddly:** I sometimes selected the lab instead of the sea.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. I was close to "Become a City", the Mine and the School had just come within reach, and growing the island was relaxing and rewarding. I'd want hiring Momos to be easier to find, and taps that land where I aim them.
