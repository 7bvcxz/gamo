## 2. What was satisfying or fun
- The Momo letters are the highlight. Research arrives as cute ideas from my workers, and "Let's research it" feels personal.
- Buying sea tiles grows the island in a way I can see.
- The chain farm → wood → mine was easy to follow because missing costs show in red.
- Watching the bunnies work on their own while coins pile up is relaxing. The day/night sky and the art are beautiful.
- The City checklist in Status gave me a clear short-term target.

## 3. What confused me or where I got stuck
- **Tapping a moving Momo** took three tries because it walks away.
- **Two ways to assign.** I couldn't tell the pencil (pick tiles) from Assign (pick a building). Assign said "Another Momo works here" on a building I never assigned, and Pomu stayed idle. I never found out who worked where.
- **Which sea tiles can I buy?** The dashed outline only appears sometimes. Later, tapping the sea sometimes offered land and sometimes did nothing.
- **The City goal is hidden in Status.** I only found it at minute 6. It should be visible earlier.
- **Small things:**
  - The "Build a research lab" button looks pressable but does nothing.
  - Tutorial bubbles sometimes cover the Momo panel text.
  - I never saw how much wood or how many flasks I had.
  - The flask counts in the research tree jumped around in ways I didn't understand.
  - The island grows off the bottom of the screen until you discover you can drag.

## 4. Would I keep playing?
Yes. I was one land tile away from "Become a City!" and wanted to see what a city unlocks. The letters make me curious what each Momo will suggest next, and the loop is calm and rewarding. Who-works-where needs to be clearer, though, especially as more Momos arrive.
