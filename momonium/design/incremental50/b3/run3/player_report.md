**2. What felt satisfying**
- The art is lovely: the sky changes through the day, the buildings look cozy, and each bunny has a name.
- Each bunny's letters are about its own job, and the research tree turns them into actual upgrades.
- Buying sea tiles and watching the island grow felt good.
- Income kept rising quickly.
- The worker panel shows small stats like "0 → 9 jobs a day" and skill levels.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- The bunny I had to tap kept moving, so my taps hit the farm or the water underneath it.
- I never saw a counter for wood or ore. I only knew I had enough wood when the Mine price changed colour.
- I never figured out what tapping "Experiment" does, or what the flags on the farms mean.
- Tile assignments seemed to change by themselves: Mochi ended up covering three tiles I hadn't given it.
- Tips piled up, and one stayed on screen after I had closed its menu.
- The bunny's info panel covered the sea tile I wanted to buy.
