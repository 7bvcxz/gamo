Here's my report as a first-time player of Momonium (v0.50.0) on the phone.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00:** The game opens on a floating island with a white bunny and a tomato bed. The prompt said "Tap the ripe crop" and I tapped it for +$4. It was already "Day 2", which seemed odd.
- **00:08–00:21:** The prompts took me through sow, then water, then harvest. The bottom bar shows Sow / Water / Harvest. A day lasts 30 seconds, so the sky went from noon to sunset to night in seconds.
- **00:29–01:02:** "Tap a Momo." I learned the bunnies are Momos. I used the pencil to give it the farm tile (1 of 4 tiles) and confirmed with the check button. A text box explained building and demolishing, and it partly covered the tile picker. After that the Momo farmed on its own and income went to $20/min.
- **01:07–01:31:** A letter from Momo arrived, "What if we widened the field?", drawn as an airmail letter. I tapped "Let's research it". The Research screen has a tree with tabs (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games…) and said I needed a research lab.
- **01:51–02:39:** I tapped an empty tile and got a build menu. The lab unlocked at Lv2, which I reached at about 02:10. I built the lab for $40 at 02:38.
- **03:00–04:09:** The lab showed "Experiment" at the bottom. At first I couldn't tell whether it ran by itself or needed a tap. Tapping it gave flasks. I started Field expansion at 04:07.
- **04:28–05:17:** I hired a second Momo, Mochi, for $60 and assigned it to the lab. Field expansion finished, so harvests went to +$8. I started Watering efficiency, which was Momo's second letter.
- **05:51–06:15:** I built a second farm on the bottom tile. A tip told me I could buy the sea next to the island. I bought it for $25 and a new grassy tile with trees appeared. That was the high point so far.
- **06:29–07:24:** I built a lumberyard ($80) and hired Pomu ($100) for it. I started Bigger harvests. The Games tab shows "Software – an island game studio" at Lv6, which made me laugh.
- **07:41–08:08:** Pomu wrote a letter about a tree nursery and I reached Lv4. My tap meant for the $35 sea tile hit Pomu's Assign button instead, because the Momo panel covered the price tag. I closed the panel and bought the tile.
- **08:22–09:01:** I built a mine ($120 + 10 wood) and hired Mugi ($155) for it. The Status screen showed my island is a "Village", with a goal to Become a City: Land 6/9, Momo 4/3 ✓, Research 2/3, Value $1K/$1.5K.
- **09:20–09:51:** I bought more land (7/9). Bigger harvests finished and income reached about $336/min. I started Sharp axes and got Mugi's letter, "Green streaks in the rock".

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The workers' letters were the best part. Each Momo proposes ideas about its own job, and every idea links straight to a node in the research tree.
- Buying sea tiles and watching the island grow, with new trees and flowers, felt great.
- The art is lovely: the day/night sky, the blueprint ghost while a building goes up, the lumberyard's stump and axe.
- Steady growth from +$4/min to +$336/min, with something new to unlock every minute.
- The City checklist on the Status screen was a clear goal, once I found it.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- The Momo info panel covers things, and twice that led to wrong taps. It hid the price tag on a sea tile, so I hit Assign instead of buying. It also overlapped the tutorial text and the tile markers.
- After assigning a Momo to the lumberyard or mine, its status line still said "the next crop is ripening".
- Mochi was assigned to the lab, but I kept seeing the bunnies standing at the farms. I couldn't tell who was actually working where.
- At first the lab's "Experiment" button just showed "5s left" and then "7s left" without moving. I couldn't tell if it was automatic or waiting for a tap.
- I never found where wood (or other materials) is counted. The mine's cost just turned from red to white at some point.
- I only found the City goal by chance at 09:00. Showing it earlier would have given me direction sooner.
- Starting on "Day 2" was puzzling.

**4. Would I keep playing?** Yes. Becoming a City is within reach (land 7/9 and research nearly 3/3), a new letter was waiting, and Smelting, Power and Games are still locked. The letters make the bunnies feel like characters, not just workers. I'm a bit worried the level curve slows down (Lv4 needs 1,200 XP), but the island-growing loop pulled me in.

**5. Confirmation:** I used only the play.mjs commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed.
