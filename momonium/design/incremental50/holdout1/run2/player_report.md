The ten minutes are up. Here is my report as the player.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:30:** I started on Day 2 on a tiny island with one farm and a bunny-like Momo. A day lasts 30 seconds and the sky goes through sunrise, sunset and night, which is lovely. The tutorial taught me tap to harvest, then sow, then water, then tap the Momo.
- **00:30–00:45:** I tapped the pencil and then the farm tile, and the Momo started working the farm on its own. Income went from +$3/min to +$9/min.
- **00:51–01:10:** The Momo writes letters, like "What if we widened the field?", with a "Let's research it" button. The research tree has tabs (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games…). Field expansion needed 3 flasks and $20, and a research lab.
- **01:17–01:56:** The lab unlocks at Lv2, so I built a second farm for $10 instead. Another letter came, about watering less often. Harvesting the second farm took me to Lv2.
- **02:11–02:53:** I built the research lab for $40. The tutorial had me buy a "glowing sea" tile for $30 to grow the island. A tip explained how to demolish buildings. A flask counter appeared.
- **03:09–03:38:** I accepted a third proposal and started Field expansion. Tapping "Next: City 0/4" showed a clear checklist: Land 5/9, Momo 1/3, Research levels 0/3, Value produced $135/$1.5K.
- **03:52–04:42:** I hired a second Momo, Mochi, for $90. Mochi took the lab tile and flasks started coming in.
- **04:47–05:24:** I built a third farm. Field expansion finished, then I started Bigger harvests ($60, 2 min).
- **05:40–06:39:** I tried to buy land, opened the farm's info panel by accident, and then bought it ($40). The new third farm sat ripe for a while because nobody tended it, so I harvested, sowed and watered it by hand.
- **06:53:** I hired a third Momo, Pomu, for $145. It went straight to the neglected farm. City 1/4.
- **07:37–07:49:** Bigger harvests finished and income jumped to about $240/min. I started Watering efficiency.
- **08:02–08:31:** I built a lumberyard ($80) and wood started coming in.
- **08:39–09:13:** I found I could drag to move the view. I bought land at $55, $70 and $90, reaching 9 tiles. City 2/4.
- **09:25–09:33:** City was 3/4 at 09:25, so the research goal had filled. I built a fourth farm. The mine needs 10 wood and I had 8.

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The art is beautiful: the day–night sky, the island and the Momos.
- The Momo letters are a charming way to suggest goals. They feel like ideas from a co-worker, not quests.
- Assigning a Momo and watching a farm run itself felt good. The upgrades paid off visibly: Bigger harvests took income from about $108 to about $240 a minute.
- The City checklist gave me a clear medium-term goal.
- Buying land and watching the island grow into a zig-zag strip was rewarding.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- **Which tiles each Momo works:** It said "1/4 tiles", but the first Momo also seemed to work the second farm. Later the third farm sat ripe with nobody on it, and Momos moved around on their own ("Manager may move"). I never knew who covered what.
- **The bottom action bar hides the land:** The Sow/Water/Harvest bar and the Momo panel sat over the glowing sea tile. Once I tapped the "Farm 1/1 ›" label and opened an info panel I didn't want.
- **Tapping empty tiles:** One tap on what looked like empty grass selected a farm instead.
- **Panning:** I didn't know I could drag the view until almost the end. Before that, new land was half off-screen.
- **A tip that didn't close:** One "Got it" tip stayed up after my first tap.
- **Research timing:** I didn't know whether research could be queued or runs one at a time, and the "Research levels" goal didn't show me how it was counted.
- **Speed:** The 30-second days go by very fast. It's hard to read what changed before night falls.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes, at least for a while. I want to finish the City, try the mine and smelting, and read more Momo letters. The loop of research, income jumps and more land is pulling me in. The main thing that would wear on me is not understanding how Momos split their work.

**5. Confirmation**
I used only the play.mjs commands (look, tap, drag, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed. I did not open or search any other file, code or documentation.
