**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The Momos are adorable, and the letters from Momo are a lovely way to unlock research. It felt like they had ideas, not like a menu.
- Watching a Momo take over the work I had done by hand was a good "it's growing" moment.
- The island stretching out into the sea one plot at a time looks nice.
- The Status checklist made the goal concrete, and racing the Value bar to the finish was exciting.
- The day/night sky changes are pretty.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- **Building:** I never got to pick where a building went; it chose the tile for me. The build menu opens from an empty tile, which I only found by accident.
- **The City goal:** it was hidden inside Status. "Next: City 0/4" sat in the corner for four minutes before I knew what the 4 things were.
- **Manual harvesting:** taps on a farm often selected the Momo standing on it instead.
- **The Momo info panel:** it covered the bottom of the island, and the only way I found to close it was tapping the sky.
- **Rounding:** "$1.5K / $1.5K" while still incomplete looked like a bug.
- **Unexplained things:** I didn't understand the little flags on the farms, "Manager may move", "Route: Nearest first", or "Special 0/1".
- **Island fit:** after expanding, the island ran off the bottom of the screen until I tried dragging.

**4. Would I keep playing?** Yes, for a while. The loop of letters, research, land and Momos is cozy and clear once Status explains it. Nation 0/4, the Mine, Lumberyard and the other research tabs all promise more. My worry is that the hands-on part fades: by the end the Momos did everything and I mostly waited. I'd want the next stage to give me more interesting choices.

**5. Confirmation:** I used only the play.mjs commands (look, tap, drag, wait, note, status) and the Read tool on the screenshots they printed. I opened no other files, did no searches, and didn't browse the web. My last note at 09:59 was sent as time ran out, so it may not have been recorded.
