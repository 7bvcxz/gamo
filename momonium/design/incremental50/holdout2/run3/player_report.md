Here's my report as the player, after a full ten minutes of Momonium (v0.50.1).

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:52:** The game opened on a cute floating island with a tomato bed and a bunny. A prompt said "Tap the ripe crop", which gave me +$3. My first "tap the farm to sow" landed on the bunny instead and opened her panel, so I tapped the other side of the bed. Then sow, water and harvest worked, each one a tap on the bed, while the bed's button bar highlighted the current step. A day lasts 30 seconds, so the sky goes from sunrise to night very quickly, which looks lovely. The tutorial then had me tap the pencil and give the farm tile to Momo. After that she worked it alone and income went to +$9/min.
- **00:58–01:13:** A mail icon appeared. It was a sweet letter from Momo suggesting "Field expansion", and I tapped "Let's research it".
- **01:19–01:35:** The Research screen is big, with tabs for Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games, Cars and more. To research I needed a lab, and labs open at Lv2. I bought a second farm for $10, and the game placed it for me.
- **02:18–02:54:** I hit Lv2 and found that tapping empty grass opens the build menu. I built the lab ($40), then the tutorial had me tap a glowing sea square to buy land ($30).
- **03:00–04:55:** I built a third farm. A second worker ("Hire") cost $90, which I couldn't afford yet. I started Field expansion (3 flasks + $20, 20 s). Income dropped from +45/min to +33/min while ripe tomatoes sat under little flags, so I sowed, watered and harvested by hand, and income recovered to +69/min.
- **05:04:** Hired Mochi, who chose its own tiles. Income passed +100/min.
- **05:26:** Started Watering efficiency. Mine research needs "Mine mastery Lv2".
- **06:03–06:20:** The Mine costs $120 plus 10 wood, so I built a Lumberyard ($80) first.
- **06:22:** I finally tapped the "Next: City 0/4" badge. It holds the real goal list: Land, Momo, Research levels and Value produced. This was the most useful screen in the game.
- **06:44–07:16:** Bought land, then hired Pomu for exactly the $145 I had.
- **07:35:** Started Field expansion Lv2.
- **08:17:** Built the Mine and income reached +$200/min. Flasks then became my limit.
- **08:57–09:44:** Bought two more land tiles and started Field expansion Lv3.

At the end I had Momo 3/3 and Research 3/3 checked, land at 8/9, value produced around $975 of $1.5K, three workers and about +$194/min.

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The art is charming: the day/night sky, the island growing tile by tile, the logs, the mine cart.
- Workers with names (Momo, Mochi, Pomu) who send letters proposing research made the upgrades feel personal.
- Sow, water, harvest is a nice little tap rhythm, and income kept rising visibly.
- Tapping empty grass to build and tapping glowing sea to buy land both felt natural.
- The City checklist gave me a clear medium-term goal.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- The first "tap the farm" hit the bunny because she stands on the bed.
- The goal badge "Next: City 0/4" sat there for six minutes before I tapped it. Nothing hinted that it opens the main goals.
- I never understood the flags on the farms. Ripe crops sat waiting while a worker stood around, and I couldn't tell if that was normal.
- New buildings were placed for me, so I never really chose where they went.
- Workers' tile assignments changed on their own ("Manager may move"), so I wasn't sure what the pencil was still for.
- Locked research said "Mine mastery Lv2" and "Lumber mastery Lv2" without saying how to raise mastery. I later spotted the per-building levels on the Status screen.
- Flasks ran short near the end, and I couldn't see how to make more besides waiting. A second lab, maybe?
- The "Special 0/1" slot in the build menu stayed a mystery.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. I was one land tile and about $500 of value away from becoming a City and wanted to see what that unlocks. The cute presentation and the steady stream of new things (lumber, mine, smelting, power…) kept me curious. My doubt is whether it stays engaging once the workers do everything, since I was mostly opening menus by the end.

**5.** I used only the play commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and the screenshots they printed. I didn't open, list or search any other file, look at code or docs, or browse the web.
