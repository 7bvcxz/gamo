## 2. What felt satisfying
- The art is lovely. The fast day/night sky, the island growing tile by tile, the mossy mine with its cart and the cabin lab all made me want to keep expanding.
- The Momo letters, written on airmail-style cards, are a charming way to suggest research ideas. "Let's research it" felt warm.
- The automation curve felt good: I tapped tomatoes for a minute, then the bunnies took over and the island ran itself. Income climbed from $3/min to about $390/min.
- The "Next: City" checklist gave me a concrete goal at the moment I was starting to wonder what to aim for.
- New Momos and buildings mostly sorted themselves out: Mugi took the lumberyard and a Momo walked into the mine. I didn't have to micromanage.

## 3. What confused me or got in my way
- **Momo blocks taps:** Momo standing on or in front of the farm caught taps meant for the plot. I opened the Momo panel by accident and once hit Assign.
- **The Momo panel blocks tiles:** it sits over the bottom of the island, so I couldn't tap the south tile until I found that tapping the sky closes it.
- **Unclear text in the Momo panel:** "Before: Research lab" and "Manager may move" made no sense to me. I never learned whether I'm meant to assign Momos or whether a manager does it. They seemed to move by themselves, which was nice but a bit mysterious.
- **Where buildings go:** the first farm I built from the menu was placed on a tile I didn't choose.
- **Fractional crops:** the Field expansion Lv2 popup said "Crops +1 → +1.25". Quarter crops felt odd.
- **Level-ups passed unnoticed:** I only saw Lv3 and Lv4 when the XP bar reset. Lv2 got a banner; the later ones may have been hidden behind open panels.
- **Mastery levels:** Sharp axes needed "Lumber mastery Lv2", and nothing pointed me to where I'd watch that progress. The Status screen shows it, but I only found that by accident.
- I never tried the Special slot, the "..." speech bubbles on Momos, or the Games tab, so I don't know what they do.

## 4. Would I keep playing?
Yes, for a while. The City checklist is a clear next goal: I still need 2 more land tiles and $1.5K value produced. I also want to see what Smelting, Power and the mine unlock, and the letters give me a small story reason to keep checking in. The main thing that would push me away is the interface getting in the way: panels covering the island and the bunnies catching my taps.
