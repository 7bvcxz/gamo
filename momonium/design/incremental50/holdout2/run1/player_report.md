The ten minutes are up. Here is my report as the player.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:30:** I opened on a cute floating island with a white bunny next to a tomato bed. The prompts walked me through it: tap the ripe crop, sow, water, then "Tap a Momo." The day/night cycle is only 30 seconds, so night came within the first half minute, which was striking.
- **00:30–00:50:** The pencil let me pick the tiles this Momo looks after. Once I gave it the farm, it farmed on its own and income rose from +$3/min to +$9/min.
- **00:52–01:20:** A mailbox letter came from Momo: "What if we widened the field?" I tapped "Let's research it." The Research screen had a tree and tabs (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games…). "Build a research lab" opened the build menu, but the lab was locked until Lv2.
- **01:28–02:00:** I built a second farm for $10 and accepted a second letter (watering efficiency). I reached Lv2 and saw that hiring a Momo costs $90.
- **02:06–02:40:** I built the research lab for $40. A prompt said "tap the glowing sea to grow the island," so I bought a sea tile for $30 and the island grew. A tip box explained building and demolishing.
- **03:00–03:35:** Momo wrote research notes at the lab, which show as flasks. I started Field expansion. Under the Games tab I found a "Software / island game studio" research, which became a fun long-term goal. Field expansion finished and harvests went from +$3 to +$6.
- **03:43:** The Status screen finally showed the City goal: Land 5/9, Momo 1/3, Research levels 1/3, Value produced $144/$1.5K. From here I had a plan.
- **04:28–04:50:** I hired Mochi for $90. Mochi went to the lab on its own, so Momo farms and Mochi researches.
- **05:03–05:25:** I compared costs. Bigger harvests gave +1 crop for 8 flasks and $60; Field expansion Lv2 gave the same for 4 flasks and $38. I picked the cheaper one.
- **05:37:** Tapping the $40 sea tile hit Mochi's panel instead and changed its route to "Row by row" by accident. I closed the panel by tapping the sky, then bought the tile.
- **06:06–06:50:** The Mine needed $120 plus 10 wood, so I built a Lumberyard ($80) first. Felling is followed by a regrow wait. I started Bigger harvests.
- **07:28–07:40:** I hired Pomu for $145, which met the Momo goal (3/3), and assigned it the lumberyard with the pencil.
- **07:59–08:13:** I accepted Mochi's "Tree nursery" letter. My first tap missed the button.
- **08:32–08:49:** I built the Mine and got my first stone.
- **09:08–09:25:** Greenhouse unlocked and I started it. Status at the end: Lv4, about $200/min, Momo 3/3, Research 3/3, Land 6/9, Value $968/$1.5K.

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The art and the fast day/night sky are lovely.
- The bunnies' letters are charming. Turning their ideas into research makes them feel like little coworkers.
- Watching income go from +$3 to +$200/min across 10 minutes felt good.
- The chain from lumberyard to wood to mine was easy to work out from the costs on the buttons.
- Growing the island by tapping the sea feels great.
- Momos moving to new buildings on their own felt alive.
- Setting the City goal list against the far-off "game studio" research gave both short- and long-term pull.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- "Next: City 0/4" did nothing when I tapped it. I only found the goals by chance, about 3.5 minutes in, under Status.
- "Build a research lab" sent me to a build menu where the lab was locked.
- The Momo/worker panel sits over the bottom of the island. One tap meant for a sea tile changed a worker's route by accident, and I had no clear close button for that panel.
- I never really understood what the route options ("Nearest first", "Row by row", "Stays here" / "Manager may move") change. I also couldn't tell why Momos left the tiles I gave them.
- Harvest pop-ups showed +$6, +$9 and +$11, and I couldn't see why they differed.
- The flask number on research nodes ("6/4", "21/11") was hard to read: is it what I have or what it costs?
- Two research nodes gave the same effect (Crops +1) at very different costs, which felt odd.
- Tutorial text sometimes overlapped the Momo panel.
- I couldn't tell whether the Lumberyard's Fell/Replant buttons were for me to tap or for the Momos.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. I was a few land tiles and about $500 of value away from becoming a City, I had just unlocked the Mine and Greenhouse, and the island-studio research is a hook I want to reach. The pace was good: something new happened every minute. My only real frustrations were finding the goals late and the panel that got in the way of tapping.
