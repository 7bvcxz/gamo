## Momonium blind playtest: report from the player (10 minutes)

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:21:** I start on a cute floating island with a bunny creature (a "Momo") and a tomato patch. The tutorial has me tap the ripe crop (+$4), then the plot to sow, then again to water, and the crop regrows within seconds. A 30-second day/night cycle runs the whole time and looks lovely.
- **00:21–00:51:** "Tap a Momo". I use the pencil to give it the farm tile, and from then on it farms by itself; coins go to +$12/min. A tip explains that any empty tile can hold a farm, mine, lumberyard or lab. Another says sea next to the island can be bought for $25.
- **00:56–01:12:** A Momo icon appears with a mail bubble. The letter is a charming handwritten proposal ("What if we widened the field?"). "Let's research it" puts it on the research list. The research tree is big (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games…). Field expansion needs 3 flasks, $20 and a research lab.
- **01:22–02:41:** I build a second farm and the Momo starts working it without being asked. Level 2 opens the lab, so I build it ($40) and press Experiment to make flasks. I accept a second letter (watering efficiency).
- **03:01–03:18:** The Momo walks into the lab on its own and keeps experimenting. I reach 3/3 flasks and Level 3 (Mine unlocked), then start Field expansion.
- **03:30–03:51:** The Mine costs $120 plus 10 wood, so I build a Lumberyard. A dashed sea plot appears and I buy it; the island grows.
- **04:01–04:50:** The Lumberyard confused me. I pressed Fell (4 sec), the tree was still there afterwards, and the timer then said "7s left". Only the Status → Store tab showed I actually had wood (6/50), plus research notes (4/30).
- **05:00–05:52:** I start Field expansion Lv2. In the Shop I hire a second Momo, "Mochi" ($50), and use the pencil to give it the lab and lumberyard.
- **06:13–07:27:** I build the Mine (income about $194/min), hire a third Momo, "Pomu" ($80), and give it the mine. I buy another sea plot and accept a Tree nursery letter.
- **07:34–08:13:** Special turns out to be landmarks (School at Lv4, Clock Tower at Lv5, Monument at Lv6). I put a farm on the new plot and research Bigger harvests (reaching Level 4).
- **08:23–09:33:** Status shows what I need to become a City: Land 6/9, Momo 3/3, Research levels 2/3, Value produced $970/$1.5K. I buy another plot. The School costs $400 + 30 wood + 20 stone, so I build a second Lumberyard instead. Pomu writes its own letter about copper. Income ends at about $300/min.

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- Income grew from $4/min to $300/min, and the island visibly grew tile by tile.
- New buildings got staffed without me doing anything. Watching the Momos walk to them felt alive.
- The letters were the highlight. Each Momo wrote in its own voice about its own job, and every letter turned into a research goal.
- The City checklist gave me a clear medium-term goal.
- The art and day/night sky are very pretty.

**3. What confused me or got in the way**
- **Lumberyard:** after Fell the tree never seemed to disappear, and the timer went from 4 sec to "7s left". Nothing on the island showed wood arriving; I had to find the Store tab.
- **Research red dot:** at 06:20 it lit up when I couldn't afford any research, so it felt misleading.
- **Prices:** the deductions didn't match the labels ($35 plot showed −$27, $80 hire showed −$72). It is probably the cost minus an $8 harvest that landed at the same moment, but it read like a wrong price.
- **Tips in the way:**
  - At 00:21 a tip covered the Momo panel.
  - "Back here with the arrow…" stayed on screen after I had left the Special menu.
  - "…p again to buy" was half hidden behind the lumberyard.
- **Assigned tile numbers** (1/2) swapped between screens. The line "Comparing: a few days are needed on each side" in the Momo panel meant nothing to me.
- **Flasks are shared:** starting one research reset every node's count to 0, which surprised me at first.
- **Night:** the darkening makes panels harder to read for a third of each 30-second day.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. The loop is easy to follow, the letters make me curious what the Momos will suggest next, and I'm one step from City, with copper and smelting coming. The resource feedback (where the wood goes) needs to be clearer for me to stay comfortable.

**5. Confirmation:** I used only the play commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed.
