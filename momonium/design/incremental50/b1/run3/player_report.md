I played until the tool said "Time is up". I finished on Day 23 at level 4 with $426 and $346/min income. I had 4 Momos, 6 land tiles and 5 buildings (2 farms, research lab, lumberyard, mine), with Greenhouse research running.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:30:** A floating island with a tomato patch and a white bunny. The HUD said "Day 3", which is odd for a first launch. The hints were "Tap the ripe crop", then tap the farm to sow, tap again to water, then "Tap a Momo". I learned the bunnies are called Momos. A whole day is 30 seconds, and the sky goes from sun to sunset to moon.
- **00:35–01:00:** The pencil lets me pick which tiles a Momo looks after (0/4). After I tapped the farm, the Momo worked on its own. A text box listed what empty tiles can become: farm, mine, lumberyard or lab. Income went from $4 to $12 to $20 a minute.
- **01:07:** A tip said I could buy the sea beside the island for $25.
- **01:13–01:50:** The Momo wrote me letters ("What if we widened the field?"), each with a "Let's research it" button. That was a charming way to bring in research. The research tree said "Build a research lab", so I realised the lab is where flasks come from.
- **02:08–03:17:** I built a lab ($40). Its experiment seemed stuck: 7s left, then still 4s left about 15 seconds later. Tapping Experiment pulled my only Momo over from the farm. Then I had 4 flasks and started Field expansion.
- **03:38–04:30:** I hired Mochi for $50. My first try to assign it with the pencil just closed the pencil mode. Assign followed by tapping the building worked.
- **04:39–05:12:** Watering efficiency research. The Mine cost $120 plus 10 wood, so I built a Lumberyard ($80) first.
- **05:51–06:22:** I hired Pomu for $80. Assign failed once; the second try worked.
- **07:18–07:30:** The Status screen was the big find. It shows a Village to City checklist: Land 4/9, Momo 3/3, Research 2/3, Value $550 of $1.5K. The Store tab is the only place I found my wood count (16/50).
- **07:50–08:12:** I built the Mine and bought a sea plot. The island visibly grew.
- **08:30–09:42:** I hired Mugi for $130 and put it on the mine. I reached level 4, built a $10 farm on the new plot, bought a second plot (the island is now an L shape) and started Greenhouse research.

**2. What felt satisfying**
- The art is lovely: the day/night cycle, the bunnies and the mossy mine with its lantern.
- Buying land and watching the island grow was the best moment.
- The Momo letters make research feel personal.
- Every few minutes there was a clear chain to follow: lab → flasks → research, and lumber → wood → mine.
- Status finally gave me a goal (become a City).
- Watching income climb from $4 to $346 a minute felt good.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- **Hidden numbers:**
  - Wood and flask counts are not on the main screen, only in Status → Store.
  - Flask counts disagreed: the research tree node said 3/3 while the side panel said 4/3.
  - Charges didn't match tags: the $120 Mine charged $112, and the $25 plot charged $19.
- **Things covered up:**
  - Tip bubbles covered the Momo panel.
  - Price tags for the sea plots were hidden behind the Building/Cancel buttons and the log pile.
  - The Momo panel has no close button and covers the bottom of the island.
  - The build menu covered the mine.
- **Assigning Momos:** Assign then tap a building only worked some of the time, and the game didn't say what Assign was waiting for. With one Momo, it kept jumping between buildings.
- **Unclear meaning:**
  - The line "Comparing: a few days are needed on each side" made no sense to me.
  - I don't know what the small orange flags on buildings mean.
  - The ring on the water appeared twice and was never explained.
  - "Lumberyard $80" showed in red when I had $82.
  - Starting on "Day 3" was confusing.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes, for a while. The loop pulls you along, and the island is cute and keeps growing. Becoming a City is a real goal, and there are many tabs I haven't opened yet (Smelting, Power, Games). What would make me drop it is the hidden numbers and the assigning that only works sometimes. Those are the two spots where I felt the game wasn't showing me what was going on.

**5. Confirmation**
I used only the play commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed. I opened no other files or folders, did not search for code or docs, and did not browse the web.
