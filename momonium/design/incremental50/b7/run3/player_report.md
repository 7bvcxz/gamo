**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The bunnies' letters are the best part. Each little idea turns into a research proposal, which makes the tech tree feel personal rather than like a menu.
- Watching income grow from $9/min to $225/min, and the island grow tile by tile, felt good.
- I enjoyed watching the bunnies walk to new buildings on their own.
- The art and the fast day/night sky are lovely.
- The City checklist gave me a clear goal once I found it.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- Tapping a building that had a Momo standing on it selected the Momo instead, which happened twice.
- My first two attempts to harvest the new farm (tapping it, then the Harvest button) didn't work, and I couldn't tell why.
- "Next: City 2/4" doesn't say which goals are done without reopening Status. After hiring a fourth Momo it stayed at 2/4, and I wasn't sure what was left. My guess is land and value produced.
- The flask counter jumped around (3 → 1 when I started a research) and I wasn't sure what it costs or gains.
- Starting on Day 2 at night was odd.
- "Build a research lab" in the Research panel opened a menu where the lab was still locked.
- Hire costs climb quickly ($90 → $145 → $235) with no warning.
- I never worked out what the "Route: Nearest first" and "Manager may move" lines mean.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes, for a while. The loop of letters, research, more money and a bigger island is cozy and keeps building on itself. Becoming a City and unlocking the School and other landmarks are concrete reasons to come back. What would make me drift off is that the bunnies do most of the work, so I sometimes felt I was just waiting for money.
