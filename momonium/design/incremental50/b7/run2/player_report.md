I played for the full ten minutes. By the end I had a 7-tile island with 3 Momos, 2 farms, a research lab, a lumberyard and a mine. I was at Lv4 with $273, earning +$210/min, and the City goal stood at 2/4.

## 1. Timeline
- **00:00** First screen: a tiny 2×2 island in the sea with a tomato plot, a tree and a white bunny. It said Day 2, 14/30 sec, $10, and "Tap the ripe crop". I tapped the plot and got +$3. The day/night cycle is only 30 seconds, which surprised me.
- **00:13–00:19** The tutorial showed me tap to sow, then tap again to water. The crop grew in about 2 seconds.
- **00:25–00:46** "Tap a Momo". The bunny is a Momo. I pressed the pencil and tapped the farm tile, which got a "1" badge. After I confirmed, Momo farmed by itself and income rose to about $9/min.
- **00:51–01:19** A letter bubble appeared over Momo and the Mailbox got a red dot. The letter, "What if we widened the field?", was a research proposal. That led me to the Research tree, which has tabs for Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games and Cars. Its "Build a research lab" button opened the build menu, where the lab was locked until Lv2.
- **01:28** I built a second farm for $10. I never picked a spot; it went onto the tile with the tree and the tree disappeared, which caught me off guard.
- **02:06–02:41** At Lv2 I tapped an empty tile and built the lab ($40). The tutorial then said "Tap the glowing sea to grow the island" and I bought land for $30.
- **03:01** A tap on a farm opened Momo's panel instead, because Momo was standing on it. It showed that Momo had already added both new buildings to its tiles.
- **03:32** I started Field expansion research (3 notes, $20, 20s).
- **04:08** "Next: City 0/4" opened the Status panel. To become a City I needed Land 5/9, Momo 1/3, Research levels 1/3 and $1.5K of value produced. From then on I knew what I was working toward.
- **04:33** I hired Mochi for $90. Mochi walked to the lab and took it over by itself.
- **05:27** Two research options had unlocked. I chose Bigger harvests ($60) over Watering efficiency ($40).
- **05:50–06:25** I bought land for $40. The Mine needed $120 plus 10 wood, so I built a Lumberyard ($80) to get wood.
- **06:45–07:00** I harvested and re-sowed farms by hand while the Momos were busy.
- **07:39** I hired Pomu for $145, completing Momo 3/3, and City went to 1/4.
- **08:07** I started Watering efficiency.
- **08:58–09:23** I built the Mine (cost $120 plus 10 wood) and got my first stone.
- **09:45** Watering efficiency finished, bringing City to 2/4. I bought another land tile for $55.

## 2. What felt satisfying or fun
- The art is lovely: the floating island, the day/night skies, the bunnies and the little mine with its minecart.
- The letters from Momo are charming. Having research arrive as a worker's idea gives the tech tree some personality.
- Workers act on their own. They pick up new buildings, and a newly hired Momo walks to an unstaffed building. Watching income climb from $3 to $210/min was a good feeling.
- The City checklist was the clearest motivation in the game, and the steady chain of new things (lab, land, lumber, mine) never let it get dull.

## 3. What confused me or where I got stuck
- **Building placement:** I never chose where a building went. The second farm replaced my tree, and the mine replaced a bush. I couldn't tell whether I was picking the tile or the game was.
- **Tapping a building selects the Momo:** at least three times I tapped a building and got the Momo standing on it instead.
- **The 30-second day** felt frantic, and the constant light changes were a bit distracting. I couldn't tell whether day and night affect anything.
- **Tile numbers** (1, 2, 3) renumbered themselves, so it took me a while to realise they show visit order.
- **Overlap:** the tutorial bubble overlapped the Momo panel at one point.
- **Never explored:** I didn't learn what the "Special 0/1" slot, "Free land" or the Life and Records tabs are for.
- **Purpose of resources:** I wasn't sure what stone or wood are for beyond the mine cost.

## 4. Would I keep playing?
Yes, for a while at least. I'm two goals away from becoming a City, the Mine and Smelting branches are just opening, and I want to read Momo's next letter. The calm idle loop with cute workers is pleasant. My worry is that later it turns into waiting on money, and the placement and selection quirks would get annoying on a busier island.
