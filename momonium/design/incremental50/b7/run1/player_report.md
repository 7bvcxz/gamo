The ten minutes are up. This is my report as a first-time player.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00** The game opened on a small floating island with a white bunny-like creature (a "Momo") next to a tomato bed. It already said Day 2, with 10 coins and Lv1. A hint said "Tap the ripe crop", so I did and got +3.
- **00:13–00:19** Hints walked me through sow, then water. Days only last 30 seconds, so night came almost immediately.
- **00:26–00:45** The hint said "Tap a Momo", then to use the pencil to pick the tiles it looks after. I assigned it to the farm and after that it farmed on its own. Income went up to about 9/min.
- **00:52–01:11** Momo wrote me a letter proposing "Field expansion", and I tapped "Let's research it". The Research screen has a big tree (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games…). That research needed 3 flasks and $20, and a button told me to build a research lab.
- **01:19–01:34** The research lab was locked until Lv2, so I built a second farm for $10. The game picked the spot itself, which I liked. Momo walked over and worked the new farm too.
- **02:03–02:27** At Lv2, tapping empty grass opened the build menu, and I built the research lab for $40.
- **02:35–02:53** "Tap the glowing sea" let me buy one more island tile for $30. A tip explained how to demolish buildings. Its first line ("Watered, the crop ripens in a moment") didn't seem to belong there.
- **03:00–03:13** Two more letters arrived, "Watering efficiency" and "Bigger harvests", and I put both on the research list. A flask counter appeared under the day.
- **03:49–04:00** With 3 flasks I started Field expansion. I reached Level 3 with confetti, and the Mine unlocked.
- **04:12–04:31** The Mine needs $120 plus 10 wood, so I'd need a Lumberyard first. I looked at "Special": these are landmarks that open from Lv4 to Lv6.
- **04:42–04:53** Harvests now paid +6. I hired a second Momo, Mochi, for $90, and it went to the lab by itself.
- **04:57–05:05** Tapping "Next: City 0/4" showed four clear goals: Land 5/9, Momo 2/3, Research levels 1/3, Value $240/$1.5K. That gave me a direction.
- **05:35** I started Bigger harvests (8 flasks, $60, 2 min).
- **06:08–06:40** I built a Lumberyard and wood started coming in. When I tried to tap the lumberyard, I selected Mochi instead, because Mochi was standing in front of it.
- **06:58** I hired a third Momo, Pomu, for $145. The price had gone up. City progress went to 1/4.
- **07:45** Bigger harvests finished and harvests paid +18. Income was about 190/min.
- **08:07–08:53** I bought another tile ($40; tapping the price label did nothing, tapping the tile itself worked). I built the Mine, started Watering efficiency, and dug my first stone.
- **09:11–09:47** Mochi wrote about a "Tree nursery". City progress was Land 6/9, Momo 3/3, Research 2/3, Value $1.1K/$1.5K. I bought one more tile for $55 and ended with 486 coins at about $300/min.

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- It's cozy and good-looking. The day/night sky is lovely and the island visibly grows.
- The letters from the Momos are charming, and each Momo writes about the job it does.
- The numbers climb fast: +3 → +6 → +18 per harvest, and income went from $3/min to $300/min in ten minutes.
- New buildings get placed for me, and Momos pick up work on their own, so I wasn't micromanaging.
- The City checklist gave me a clear medium-term goal.

**3. What confused me or got in the way**
- The Momo info panel stayed open at the bottom for a long time and covered part of the island. I never found a clean way to close it.
- Tapping a building often selected a Momo standing in front of it.
- Several tips and popups stacked up at once (level-up, sea tile, tip box) and covered the island.
- Tapping the "$40" price label under the sea tile did nothing. Only the tile itself works.
- I never understood what "Stays here" vs "Manager may move" and "Route: Nearest first" really change.
- It wasn't obvious that tapping empty grass opens the build menu. I found it by trying.
- Starting on "Day 2" was slightly odd.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. I was close to "Become a City!", I had just unlocked the Mine with Smelting and Power waiting in the tree, and the Momos' letters make me want to see what comes next.

**5. Confirmation**
I used only the play commands (look, tap, drag, wait, note, status) and the Read tool on the screenshots they printed. I did not open, list or search any other file, and I did not look at code, docs or the web.
