My ten minutes are up. Here's my report as a first-time player.

## 1. Timeline
- **00:00–00:33** A tiny floating island at night with a vegetable bed and a bunny. The hints walked me through it: "Tap the ripe crop" (+$3), then "Tap the farm to sow" and "Tap again to water". A whole day goes by in 30 seconds, so the sky kept flipping between day, sunset and night. At 00:14 I tapped the bunny ("Momo") by mistake because it had walked onto the bed.
- **00:39–00:53** The hint had me tap the pencil and pick the tiles Momo looks after. I picked the farm and confirmed, and Momo started harvesting by itself. Income went from $3/min to $9/min.
- **00:59–01:13** A letter from Momo ("What if we widened the field?") suggested a research topic. I liked this. I pressed "Let's research it".
- **01:20–01:36** The research tree has tabs: Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games… "Field expansion" needed 3 flasks and $20. The button "Build a research lab" opened a build menu, but the lab only opens at Lv2, so I built a second farm for $10.
- **01:55** I harvested by hand and reached **Level 2** with confetti. That unlocked the Research lab and the Lumberyard.
- **02:09–03:05** I learned that tapping empty grass opens the build menu. I built the lab for $40, accepted a second letter (Watering efficiency) and grew the island by tapping the glowing sea ($40, tap again to confirm). Flasks started appearing on their own.
- **03:30** I started Field expansion. At 03:51 I found that "Next: City 0/4" opens a Status panel with clear goals: Land 5/9, Momo 1/3, Research levels 1/3, Value $135/$1.5K. That gave me a direction.
- **04:00–05:00** I reached Lv3 and harvests paid $6. I saved up by harvesting myself and hired a second bunny, "Mochi", for $100.
- **05:17** I tried to give Mochi a farm and got "Another Momo works here". Momo had already claimed every building.
- **05:26–06:09** The Mine needs $120 plus 10 wood, so I chose the Lumberyard ($80). Its price stayed red and tapping did nothing, even with $97 and then $115. I closed the menu and reopened it, and the price turned white and the build worked. That cost me about 20 seconds.
- **06:28–08:01** I researched Field expansion Lv2 and accepted a third letter (Bigger harvests). I bought another land tile for $55, then started Bigger harvests (2 minutes). Harvests were paying $9.
- **08:14–09:15** I found that Mochi was still on "Free time" after about 3 minutes. I built a farm on the new land and opened its detail panel. It has crop types, refits (Villa, Park, Pub) and stats. Tapping the "Empty" worker slot did nothing. I reached Lv4.
- **09:38** I ended with $178, 16 wood, 5 flasks, Lv4. Mochi was still not working.

## 2. What felt satisfying
- The art is lovely. The island grows, the sky turns over, and the bunnies smile and show hearts.
- Momo's letters are the best part. Research feels like *their* idea, and it's a nice way to point me at the next upgrade.
- Upgrades showed up quickly as money: $3, then $6, then $9 a harvest, plus fast level-ups with confetti.
- The City checklist in Status gave me a clear medium-term goal.
- The "tap again to buy" confirm on land felt safe.

## 3. What confused me or where I got stuck
- **I never got the second bunny working.** Assign said "Another Momo works here", the farm's "Empty" slot did nothing, and Momo quietly took the new lumberyard. I paid $100 for a bunny that sat idle for about 5 minutes.
- **The two bunnies look identical.** I couldn't tell Momo from Mochi, and I kept selecting the wrong one.
- **Bunnies get in the way of taps.** They walk onto the building I'm tapping, so I selected a bunny instead of the farm four or five times.
- **The build menu's prices don't update while it's open.** The Lumberyard showed a red price and wouldn't build even though I had enough money.
- **The "Next: City" label moves.** It shifted down when the flask counter appeared, so my first tap missed it.
- **Farms sat ripe a lot.** I didn't understand how Momo picks what to do next ("Route: Nearest first").
- **Toasts overlap things.** A "Researched" message covered the tab row in the Status panel.
- I didn't know where the flasks came from. They just appeared.

## 4. Would I keep playing?
Yes, probably for another session. The look, the letters and the City goal pull me in, and there's clearly a lot to unlock (mine, smelting, power, villas, pubs). But if I still couldn't put a second worker to work, I'd get frustrated quickly, because hiring more bunnies seems to be the whole idea.

## 5. Confirmation
I used only the play commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and opened only the screenshots they printed. I didn't open, list or search any other file, code or documentation, and I didn't browse the web.
