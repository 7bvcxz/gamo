**Player report: momonium (v0.50.0), first 10 minutes**

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:23**: I opened on a small floating island with a vegetable bed and a white bunny. Prompts walked me through it: tap the ripe crop (+$3, some XP), tap the farm to sow, tap again to water. A whole day lasts 30 seconds, so night came almost right away. The crop ripened in a couple of seconds.
- **00:25–00:48**: "Tap a Momo." The bunny is a Momo. I used the pencil to give it the farm tile. After that it farmed on its own: 6 jobs, and income went from $3/min to $9/min.
- **00:54–01:09**: A mail icon appeared over the Momo. The letter ("What if we widened the field?") proposed a research topic, and I tapped "Let's research it".
- **01:16–01:35**: The Research tree needs flask points, coins and a research lab, and the lab only opens at Lv2. Instead I built a second farm for $10. It went onto a free tile by itself and was planted at once.
- **01:43–02:20**: A second letter (watering efficiency) went onto the research list. I reached Lv2 and learned that tapping empty grass opens the build menu. I built the research lab for $40. The Momo started doing "Experiment" there, which earns flasks.
- **02:28–03:07**: A hint said "The island can grow here, $40". Tapping the glowing sea twice (once to see the price, once to buy) added a tile. A third letter (bigger harvests) arrived.
- **03:43**: I started Field expansion. Harvests went from +$3 to +$6.
- **03:55–04:24**: Hire costs $100. "Next: City 0/4" opened a Status screen with a clear checklist: Land 5/9, Momo 1/3, Research levels 1/3, Value $162/$1.5K. That gave me a real goal.
- **04:48–05:26**: I hired Mochi and gave it both farms. The Momo panel shows "Since the change: 0 → 6 jobs a day", which I liked.
- **05:56–06:21**: I started Field expansion Lv2 and bought a sea tile for $55.
- **06:45–07:09**: I built a Lumberyard ($80); wood began coming in. I started Bigger harvests (2 min).
- **07:49–08:08**: I hired Pomu for $160, and City went to 1/4. I found that "Assign" means tap one building, while the pencil means pick several tiles. Pomu went to the lumberyard.
- **08:38–09:09**: I bought another tile ($70, so 7 of 9 land) and built a Mine ($120 plus 10 wood). It was digging right away. Bigger harvests finished, and City went to 2/4.
- **09:34**: I started Watering efficiency and got my first stone. I ended at about $188 and +$224/min.

**2. What felt good**
- Everything I bought turned into visible new things on the island within seconds.
- The Momos' letters are charming. Turning one into a research topic feels personal.
- The City checklist gave me a clear goal for the middle of the session.
- Income kept climbing: $3/min, then $45, then $132, then $224.
- The day/night cycle is pretty, and the bunnies are cute.

**3. What confused me**
- **Tip text overlapping panels:** the "pick the tiles" tip covered the Momo panel's name and status.
- **Tap went through a tip:** at about 04:25 my "Got it" tap opened the build menu on the empty tile behind the tip.
- **Odd tip text:** one tip opened with "Watered, the crop ripens in a moment" on top of building instructions that had nothing to do with watering.
- **Flask count looked wrong:** the flask number dropped from 3 to 1 when I started research, and for a moment I wasn't sure what had happened.
- **"Research levels":** I couldn't tell whether this counts levels or different topics, so I wasn't sure which research would move the City goal.
- **Where buildings go:** the build menu covers the tile you tapped, so I couldn't see where a building would land.
- **Pencil vs Assign:** I only worked out the difference by trying both.
- **Night tint:** it hides a lot of detail. Momos and icons become hard to read.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes, for a while. I was two steps from becoming a City (land and value produced), and I want to see what a City unlocks. I'm also curious about the Smelting, Power and Games research tabs. The loop of "get mail, research, expand" is pleasant. Whether I stayed for good would depend on more happening after the City than numbers going up.

**5. Rules**
I used only the play commands (look/tap/wait/note/status) and opened only the screenshots they printed with Read.
