My ten minutes are up. This is my report as the player.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:20:** I started on a small floating island with a tomato patch and a bunny. Following the prompts, I harvested, sowed and watered. A full day and night passes in 30 seconds, which surprised me. The crops ripened again within a couple of seconds.
- **00:25–00:50:** It told me to tap a Momo (the bunny), then use the pencil to pick the tiles it looks after. I gave it the farm and it started farming on its own: +$3 and +3 EXP each harvest, and income went up to $9/min.
- **00:51–01:20:** Envelopes appeared over the farm and on the Mailbox. They were postcards from Momo suggesting research ("What if we widened the field?"), and I tapped "Let's research it". The research tree has many tabs (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games, Cars…) and told me I needed a research lab, which opens at Lv2.
- **01:28–02:47:** I built a second farm for $10. At Lv2 I built the lab for $40, then bought my first sea tile for $40. Buying works as "tap the glowing sea, then tap again".
- **03:30–04:30:** I built a third farm and started Field expansion (crops +1). All three farms were ripe while my one Momo sat in the lab, so I harvested by hand.
- **05:06–05:50:** I hired Mochi for $100. Assigning was confusing (see 3), so I used the pencil instead and gave Mochi two farms. Income rose to about $100/min.
- **05:55:** Tapping "Next: City 0/4" showed the requirements: Land 5/9, Momo 2/3, Research levels 1/3, Value $330/$1.5K. After that I knew what I was working toward.
- **06:25–07:20:** I researched Bigger harvests ($60 + 8 flasks). I bought land at $55. The Mine needed $120 plus 10 wood, so I built a Lumberyard ($80) first. A Momo walked over and started felling.
- **07:35:** I looked at Special. These are landmarks: Clock Tower (Lv5), School ($400 + wood + stone) and Momo Monument (Lv6).
- **07:58–08:50:** I hired Pomu for $160, which made the City goal 1/4. Bigger harvests finished, so each harvest gave $9 and income was about $200/min. I started Watering efficiency. Mochi wrote asking for a tree nursery.
- **09:20–09:55:** I bought land at $70. Wood reached 10, so I built the Mine with my last seconds. A Momo went straight in to Dig. I ended at Lv4 with 7 land tiles, 3 Momos and about $230/min.

**2. What was fun**
- Watching the bunnies take over the work I'd been tapping by hand. The +$ and +EXP pop-ups made each step feel like progress.
- The postcard letters from the Momos are charming, and turning a letter into research ("Let's research it") felt personal.
- The island growing tile by tile, the fast day/night sky and the art all look great.
- The City checklist gave me a clear medium-term goal. The Mochi card's "Since the change: 0 → 10.5 jobs a day" showed me my change had worked.

**3. What confused me or got me stuck**
- **Assigning a hired Momo:** twice I tapped a building for a new Momo and got "Another Momo works here". The bunnies all look the same, so I couldn't tell which one was Mochi or Pomu, or who was where.
- **The Momo panel covers the bottom of the island:** I couldn't tap the lumberyard to assign Pomu to it.
- **Momos move around on their own:** one appeared in the lab and another left the lumberyard. I wasn't sure whether my assignments were being kept.
- **Tapping a farm with a Momo on it:** I meant to harvest but selected the bunny instead.
- **Unclear costs:** I didn't know what flasks were until the lab started making them. The Mine's need for wood only showed up in the build menu. Research costs went up between levels ($20 → $36 for Field expansion).
- **The fast day/night cycle:** every other screenshot is dark, which made the farms harder to read.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes, for a while. The loop of more farms, more bunnies, research and more land works, the next goal was always visible, and I want to see the island become a City and build a landmark. If the Momo-assigning confusion continued as the island grew, though, it could start to annoy me.
