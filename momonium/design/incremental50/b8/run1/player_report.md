**2. What felt satisfying**
- The art is lovely, and the 30-second day/night cycle makes the island feel alive.
- The letters from the Momos asking to research things are charming. They make the upgrades feel like the Momos' own ideas.
- Watching my income climb from $3/min to about $318/min was satisfying.
- Growing the island tile by tile into a long winding shape felt good.
- The City checklist with green ticks gave me a clear target and a strong "almost there" pull at the end.

**3. What confused me or got me stuck**
- The most useful screen, the City goal list, sits behind a small "Next: City 0/4" label. I only found it at 06:05. Before that I was spending without a plan.
- The Research button sent me to "Build a research lab" while the lab was still locked. That was a dead end at first.
- Pressing "Got it" on a tip also closed the build menu underneath it, so my next tap missed. I lost the farm I was trying to buy.
- When I opened the build menu, nothing showed which tile the building would go on. The menu also covers the island.
- Momos labelled "Manager may move" wander between jobs. For a while the lab had nobody in it, and I couldn't tell whether research was still progressing.
- "Research levels 1/3" was unclear: does it count levels or different research topics?
- The bottom panels (a Momo's info card, the farm action bar) often covered the glowing sea I wanted to tap. I had to scroll the view to reach the bottom of the island.
- I never worked out what "Special 0/1" or the Store, Games and Life tabs are.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. The loop of reading letters, researching, expanding and hiring is gentle and rewarding. I ended exactly at the moment I could become a City, and I really want to see what a City unlocks: the Smelting, Power and Games tabs and the "landmark slot". I'd be happier if the City goals were visible from the start.

**5. Rules**
I used only the play commands (look, tap, drag, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed. I didn't open, search or browse anything else.
