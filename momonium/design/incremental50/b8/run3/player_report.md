I played the full ten minutes. I ended on Day 22 at Level 4, with 3 Momos, 5 buildings, 6 of 9 land tiles, income of about $103/min, and the "Next: City" goal at 2/4.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:48:** I start on a tiny night-time island with a tomato plot and a bunny-like creature. "Tap the ripe crop" gives +$3 and the night turns into day. The prompts then walk me through Sow, Water, "Tap a Momo" and the pencil to pick the Momo's tiles. I find out a day is only 30 seconds. After I assign the farm tile, Momo farms on its own (+$9/min).
- **00:54–01:21:** A mail bubble shows up. It's a sweet letter from Momo, "What if we widened the field?", with a "Let's research it" button. The research tree has tabs (Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games…). Field expansion needs 3 flasks, $20 and a research lab.
- **01:30:** "Build a research lab" opens a build menu, but the lab is locked until Lv2, so I buy a second farm ($10). The game picked the tile for me.
- **02:02:** Level 2 with fireworks: Research lab and Lumberyard unlock. I figure out that tapping empty grass opens the build menu, and build the lab ($40).
- **02:26–02:35:** "Tap the glowing sea to grow the island." Tap once to see the price, tap again to buy. The island grows.
- **03:42:** I start Field expansion. Once it lands, a harvest pays $6 instead of $3, which felt great.
- **03:54–04:04:** Level 3 opens the Mine. The Status panel gives me a real goal: become a City (Land 5/9, Momo 1/3, Research levels, Value $144/$1.5K).
- **04:36:** I hire a second Momo, "Mochi", for $90. Mochi goes to the lab by itself.
- **05:18:** I start Field expansion Lv2. Its toast says Crops +1 → +1.25, a much smaller bump than Lv1.
- **05:40:** I buy a second sea tile ($40).
- **05:59:** The Mine turns out to need $120 plus 10 Wood, so I build a Lumberyard ($80) first. A Momo leaves the farms to fell trees.
- **06:28–08:48:** The farms are often ripe with no Momo nearby, so I spend a lot of time harvesting and sowing by hand. The Harvest button in the bottom bar is easier to hit than the farm, because Momos stand in the way.
- **06:59:** I start Bigger harvests (8 flasks, $60, 2 minutes). Then I learn only one research can run at a time.
- **08:10:** I build the Mine. A third Momo now costs $145.

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- The art is lovely: the sky shifts through day, sunset and night every 30 seconds, and the island visibly grows.
- The letters from Momo are charming. A worker suggesting the next upgrade is a great way to lead into research.
- Upgrades pay off where I can see them. The first Field expansion doubling the harvest was a real "aha".
- Each new Momo has a name and walks off to a job by itself.
- There was always a next goal, and the City checklist gave the session a direction.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- The game placed my second farm itself; I never chose where it went.
- "Build a research lab" opened a menu where the lab was locked, which felt like a dead end.
- Momos wander between buildings, and I often couldn't tell who was assigned where. The farms sat ripe and unattended a lot, and I had to farm myself.
- Momos standing on the farms made my taps hit the Momo instead of the crop. One harvest tap opened Momo's panel instead.
- Field expansion Lv2 cost more and gave much less (+0.25 vs +1). That was a let-down.
- I couldn't tell that only one research runs at a time until I tried. Meanwhile I had 20+ flasks piling up.
- I didn't know the Mine needed wood until I opened the build menu.
- The Smelting icon in Status is a sprout, which looks like farming.
- The game reached Level 4 without the big banner the earlier levels had (I may have missed it).
- "Next: City 2/4" didn't tell me which conditions were already met unless I opened Status.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. The loop of earning, building, hiring a Momo, reading a letter and researching kept me busy all ten minutes, and the City goal was a clear thing to chase. I'd be more eager if the Momos kept the farms running better, so I could make decisions instead of tapping crops.

**5. Confirmation**
I used only the play commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and the Read tool on the screenshots they printed. I didn't open, list or search any other file, code or documentation.
