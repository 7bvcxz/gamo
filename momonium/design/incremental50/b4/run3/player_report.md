Here's my report on the ten minutes, as the player.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:35:** I opened on a pretty floating island with a bunny, a tomato patch and "Tap the ripe crop". My first tap hit the bunny, because it had walked in front of the tomatoes, and opened a "Momo / Assign" panel. My second tap harvested (+$4). The prompts then walked me through Sow and Water, then "Tap a Momo". A day is only 30 seconds long, so day and night went by very quickly.
- **00:42–01:05:** I used the pencil to give Momo the farm tile, and it started farming on its own: income +$8/min, rising to +$20/min.
- **01:11–01:41:** A letter from Momo, "What if we widened the field?", led into research. The research screen said "Build a research lab". I tapped it and nothing happened; it's a greyed-out hint, not a button.
- **01:50–02:46:** I tapped an empty tile and got a build menu. I built a second farm, reached Lv2, and learned I can buy sea tiles. I built the research lab for $40.
- **02:54–03:44:** I hired Mochi for $60, put Mochi on the lab, collected flasks and started Field expansion.
- **03:53–04:42:** More letters arrived, each tied to a research node: Watering efficiency and Bigger harvests. I started Watering efficiency.
- **04:52–05:40:** The Mine needed $120 + 10 wood, so I built a Lumberyard first. I hired Pomu for $100, tried to assign Pomu to the Lumberyard and got "Another Momo works here".
- **05:50:** The Status screen finally gave me a clear goal: Village → City, which needs Land 4/9, Momo 3/3, Research 1/3 and Value $470/$1.5K.
- **06:12–07:08:** I bought a land tile for $25, started Bigger harvests, built the Mine and reached Lv4.
- **07:15–08:02:** A letter from Mochi proposed a Tree nursery. I dug once by hand and bought a second land tile for $35.
- **08:19–09:39:** I looked at Special (landmarks: Clock Tower, School, Momo Monument). I built a third farm, hired Mugi for $155 and assigned Mugi to that farm. I started Greenhouse research. By the end, City needed only Land (6/9) and $0.3K more value. Income was $300/min.

**2. What felt good**
- The snowball: income went from +$4 to +$300 a minute in ten minutes.
- Momo's letters, each turning into a research proposal. It's a warm way to introduce the tech tree.
- Watching the island grow when I bought land.
- Mochi's card showing "Since the change: 0 → 4 jobs a day" after I assigned it.
- The City checklist, once I found it, gave me a real goal.

**3. What confused me**
- My very first tap opened the bunny's panel instead of harvesting.
- "Build a research lab" looks like a button but does nothing.
- I never knew who was working where. Pomu got "Another Momo works here" on the Lumberyard, the Mine showed 1/1 and then 0/1, and Momos wandered between buildings. I couldn't tell whether assigning mattered.
- Mochi's status said "the next crop is ripening" even though I'd given it the lab.
- The City goal is hidden in Status. I only found it by accident at about 6 minutes, and it would have guided me from the start.
- Worker panels cover the land tile and its price tag.
- Several pop-up tips stacked up at once (sea tiles, the pencil, the bin).

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes. I was three land tiles from becoming a City, the landmarks were the next carrot, and new letters kept arriving. It's relaxing and the rewards come quickly. What would stop me later is the worker assignment, because I couldn't tell what my choices actually changed.

**5. Confirmation:** I used only the play commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed.
