The ten minutes are over. Here's my report as the player.

**1. Timeline**
- **00:00–00:20:** A cute floating island with a bunny (a "Momo") and a tomato patch. I followed the hints: harvested (+4 coins), sowed and watered. Day and night cycle about every 30 seconds.
- **00:25–00:55:** Tapped the Momo, used the pencil to give it the farm tile, and it started farming by itself. Then a long popup appeared about building a farm, mine, lumberyard or lab and how to tear things down. That was a lot to read at once.
- **01:02–01:30:** The Mailbox had a letter from Momo proposing "Field expansion". I loved this. I pressed "Let's research it", and the research tree opened with tabs for Farm, Lumber, Mine, Smelting, Power, Games and more. The "Build a research lab" button looked like a shortcut but was just a greyed label; nothing happened when I tapped it.
- **01:40–02:36:** Tapping empty grass opened the build menu. The lab was locked until level 2, so I built a second farm. I hit Level 2 with confetti and built the lab for $40. One of my "Got it" taps went through to a sea tile behind it and selected it for purchase.
- **02:45–04:30:** Hired Mochi for $60. Assigning Mochi to the lab was confusing. My tap landed on the empty tile next to it, and Mochi showed "On its rounds – the next crop is ripening" while the lab showed a "?". I fixed it by tapping the lab door. Started Field expansion once I had 3 flasks.
- **04:47–06:11:** Field expansion levels 1 and 2 raised income from about $60 to $172/min. The mine needed $120 plus 10 wood, so I built a lumberyard. Mochi picked it up on its own because of the tile I'd assigned by accident. Started "Bigger harvests" (2 minutes).
- **06:18–07:36:** Bought a sea tile for $25 and land rose up out of the water, which was satisfying. My next tap selected another sea tile ($35) instead of the new land. At wood = 10 I built the mine, reached Level 4, hired Pomu for $100 and put Pomu on the mine.
- **07:46:** The Status screen finally gave me a clear goal: Village → City. It needs land 5/9, Momo 3/3 (done), research levels 2/3 and value produced $832/$1.5K.
- **08:18–09:45:** Bought another tile and started Watering efficiency. Under Special I found landmarks: Clock Tower, School ($400 + 30 wood + 20 stone) and Momo Monument. Built a third farm and hired Mugi for $155. I ended at about $620 and $338/min.

**2. What felt satisfying or fun**
- Letters from my Momo that turn into research. It feels personal.
- Watching the bunnies take over the work and income tick up.
- Land rising out of the sea when I buy a tile.
- The day/night colours and the building art (the blue-roofed lab, the mine burrow with its lantern).
- Each resource clearly unlocked the next thing: wood for the mine, then wood and stone for the School.

**3. What confused me**
- Assigning tiles on the small isometric island was the biggest problem. Buildings overlap neighbouring tiles, so taps hit the wrong one. It happened with the lab vs. the grass, the new land vs. the sea, and "Got it" vs. the sea tile.
- After assigning, I couldn't easily tell which tiles a Momo had.
- "Build a research lab" looks like a button but does nothing.
- The Village → City goal is hidden in Status. I only found it at 07:46; I'd have played toward it from the start.
- "Research levels 2/3": I couldn't tell what counts toward it.
- Momo panels covered the buyable sea tile, and I didn't know how to close them.
- I never learned what "Route: Nearest first" or "Stays here" mean.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Yes, for a while. It's relaxing, the bunnies are charming, and I now have clear goals: become a City and build the School. If tile picking stays this fiddly I'd get annoyed. I'd also want the City goal shown on the main screen.

**5. Confirmation**
I used only the play commands (look, tap, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed. I opened no other files, code or docs.
