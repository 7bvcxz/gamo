The ten minutes are over. Here is my report as the player.

## 1. Timeline
- **00:00–00:30:** The game opens on a small floating island with a garden bed and a white bunny. Prompts walked me through it: tap the ripe crop, tap to sow, tap again to water, then "Tap a Momo." A whole day (sun, sunset, moon) passes in about 30 seconds, which is lovely to watch.
- **00:27–00:54:** The pencil let me pick the tiles the Momo looks after. I chose the farm and it started farming on its own. A text box listed what can go on empty tiles: farm, mine, lumberyard, lab. It was a lot to read at once.
- **00:59–01:28:** I opened the mailbox. The letter from Momo, "What if we widened the field?", is charming and acts as a research suggestion. Research needs flasks, and the "Build a research lab" line looked like a button but did nothing when I tapped it.
- **01:37–02:10:** Tapping an empty tile opened a build menu. I built a second farm for $10. I reached level 2, and a tip said I could buy the sea next to the island for $25.
- **02:18–03:04:** I built a research lab ($40) and hired a second Momo, Mochi ($60), and used the pencil to assign Mochi to the lab.
- **03:21–04:09:** I researched Field expansion, then Watering efficiency. Hit level 3. More letters came in, each suggesting the next research.
- **04:21–04:31:** A mine needs $120 plus 10 wood, with the wood shown in red, so I worked out I needed a lumberyard first and built one ($80).
- **05:02–05:30:** I hired a third Momo, Pomu ($100), and tried the Assign button. Both the lumberyard and the left farm said "Another Momo works here." Then a tap on the farm selected the Momo standing there instead of assigning Pomu, and numbered circles 1, 2, 3 appeared on tiles that I never understood.
- **05:41:** The Status screen gave me a clear goal: Village to City, needing land 9, Momo 3, research levels 3 and $1.5K in value produced.
- **06:00–06:19:** I bought two sea plots ($25, then $35). Watching the island grow was very satisfying.
- **06:36–07:02:** Started Bigger harvests and reached level 4. Built a mine.
- **07:50–08:31:** Income kept falling (from $176/min down to about $122/min). Ripe farms sat unharvested while the bunnies crowded around the mine and lumberyard. I hired a fourth Momo, Mugi ($155), but assigning failed the same way, so I sowed, watered and harvested the top farm by hand. Dragging the screen did not move the view.
- **09:00–09:47:** Status showed Momo 4/3 and research 3/3 done, land 6/9 and value $972/$1.5K. I bought land plots 7 ($45) and 8 ($55), so I was one plot and some value short of becoming a City.

## 2. What felt satisfying or fun
- The art: the day/night sky, the cute bunnies, the little buildings, and heart bubbles over the Momos.
- Letters from the bunnies proposing research. It makes them feel like they have ideas.
- Buying sea plots and seeing the island grow.
- The City checklist on the Status screen gave me a goal to work toward.
- The red cost on the mine taught me the lumberyard → mine order without any explanation.

## 3. What confused me or where I got stuck
- **Who works where:** I couldn't see which building had a worker without tapping it. I only learned a building takes one Momo when I got "Another Momo works here."
- **Pencil vs. Assign:** I didn't understand the difference. When I tapped a building while assigning, I often selected the Momo or the building instead, and I never knew whether Pomu or Mugi actually got a job.
- **Idle farms:** Income dropped while farms sat ripe, and nothing told me why.
- **Numbered circles:** The 1, 2, 3 circles on tiles were never explained.
- **Fake button:** "Build a research lab" in the research panel looks like a button but does nothing.
- **Hidden plots:** The next sea plot sat under or below the bottom buttons, and dragging didn't pan the view.
- **Text box:** The early text about farm, mine, lumberyard and lab was a lot to read at once.

## 4. Would I keep playing?
Yes, at least for a while. I was one plot and about $500 of value away from the City upgrade and wanted to see it. The letters and the growing island pull me in. The worker-assignment confusion and income dropping for no visible reason were the main frustrations. If they continued I would start to feel I wasn't in control.
