> **What the game is:** A cozy clockwork idle/clicker game set in a tailor's workshop at night. You click or hold the big brass gear to spin a golden bobbin and earn spirals. A glowing thread links the bobbin to one sleeping wind-up doll at a time, and a ring by its key fills as you earn. When the ring completes, some spirals are spent, the doll wakes up, and it joins the crew winding the next sleeper. Each wake also raises income and adds something to the room:
> - After 1 doll: a small gear and a desk lamp.
> - After 2: the sewing machine was uncovered.
> - After 3 and 4: more gears, and the half-sewn dress form became a finished jacket.
> 
> **What I understood:**
> - Holding the gear works much better than single clicks, at about 5 spirals per second early on and about 15 later.
> - Awake dolls produce passive income (about 3 per second with two awake).
> - Each wake costs more than the last.
> - The workshop gradually "comes to life" as a visual progress meter.
