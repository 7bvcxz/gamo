> **What I think the game is:** a calm, moody idle/clicker set in a toymaker's workshop at night. You turn a large brass wind-up key. Each turn gives you "spirals," the currency shown at the top. A thread links the key to one sleeping doll, and your winding slowly fills a ring around that doll's key. When the ring is full, the doll wakes up, a fixed amount of spirals is spent, and a new small gear attaches to the machine. Each doll that wakes adds passive income and then walks over to help wind the next sleeper, so progress speeds up the more dolls you wake. The workshop also changes as you go: a desk lamp lit up, and a covered shape became a glowing red sewing machine.

> **What I understood:** clicking or holding the key earns spirals. Holding seems to work as a steady stream of clicks. Dolls wake on their own once their ring fills, and the game picks which doll comes next. Spirals kept going up between wake-ups even when I wasn't touching anything.
