> **What I understood**
> - **Earning:** clicking the brass gear gives 1 spiral. Holding it down earns faster, about 4 per second.
> - **Goal:** a thread runs from the gear to one sleeping wind-up doll at a time, and a ring around that doll's key shows its progress.
> - **Waking dolls:** when the ring fills, the spirals are spent automatically and the doll wakes up in color. There was no shop or upgrade menu.
> - **What each wake-up adds:** another gear on the main machine, which seems to add passive income (about 2 per second after the first one), and sometimes a workshop item: first the desk lamp, then the sewing machine under the dust sheet.
> - **Awake dolls help:** they walk over to the next doll and wind it with you, which makes the room feel alive.
> - **Progress:** I woke 3 of roughly 14 dolls and ended with four gears and 272 spirals.
> 
> **What confused me**
> - **Hidden costs:** the game never shows how much the next doll costs. I could only tell spirals had been spent when the counter suddenly dropped.
> - **Things that did nothing:** clicking the dolls, the sewing machine and the glowing face-orb had no visible effect. The orb might be a companion or a later feature.
> - **Unknown icons:** I didn't try the sparkle icon at the top left (maybe settings or a prestige option) or the circular-arrow icon at the top right (probably restart).
> - **The clock:** the grandfather clock's hands moved between screenshots, which might be a hidden timer for the night.
> 
> **Would I keep playing?**
> Yes, for a while. The art and the slow lighting-up of the workshop are charming, and watching the dolls help each other is a nice touch. But the only real decision so far is "hold the gear," and the wake-ups are getting slower. I'd keep going only if the clock, the orb or the unused icons turn out to add real choices.
