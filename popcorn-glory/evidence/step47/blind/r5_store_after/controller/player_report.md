**2. What was fun and what wasn't**
- **Fun:** The hold-and-release timing feels great. The golden burst, the circles filling up and the explosion are very satisfying. Every upgrade visibly changes the machine or room, which I loved. Getting gold in the challenge was the high point.
- **Fun:** It's a nice puzzle that each upgrade moves the bottleneck to another room, and the red outline on the slow room is a helpful hint.
- **Not fun:** After about 10 minutes, much of the time went to waiting for coins while the machine sat grey and blocked. The cooking I enjoyed most kept getting switched off.

**3. What confused me or where I got stuck**
- The grey "blocked" machine. My first challenge stalled, and I only found out why after closing the newspaper, which was covering the rooms that were full.
- The gadgets (fan, lever, mini machine) and their numbers ("0 &gt; 19", "-4", "-1", green check versus grey check). I still don't know what my fan does.
- The overview panel's rates and the "+2k" slots. There were a lot of numbers and no way to tell what they meant.
- The second trophy challenge (serve 42/52/62 customers). Its button showed a newspaper and later a padlock, then the card disappeared from the list.
- The sparkle button only toggled its icon. I couldn't tell what it changed.
- The coins seemed to stop while the newspaper or the gadget picker was open. I wasn't sure if those pause the game.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Probably for a while longer, yes. I want the next packer upgrade, the other gadgets and the customer challenge. But if the game keeps being mostly waiting with a blocked machine, I'd likely drift off. The skill part needs to stay active more of the time.
