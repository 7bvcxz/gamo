I played the popcorn shop game for the full twenty minutes. Here's my report as the player.

**1. Timeline**
- **0:00–0:13** First look: a popcorn shop in three stacked sections. At the top is a counter with a queue of customers. In the middle is a kitchen with a filler and a belt of buckets. At the bottom is a big red popcorn cart inside a coloured ring, with a grid of about 24 small machines beside it. There was a yellow upgrade button (2,500 coins, +23 → +29) and 10,108 coins.
- **0:18–1:26** Tapping the cart did little. Holding it for 2 seconds filled 2 of the 8 dots under the cart. Holding for 3 seconds made black smoke, which I read as burnt. When all 8 dots filled, popcorn burst out of the cart.
- **1:37–1:52** I bought two cart upgrades. Each time the cart got bigger, and the price climbed: 5,800, then 14,000. Once I couldn't afford the next one, the button greyed out and showed a fill bar of my progress.
- **2:04–2:47** The newspaper had a news tab (timed rushes: "customers ×1.8" with a countdown) and a trophy tab with challenges. Each challenge had bronze, silver and gold targets and a small entry fee. The "?" on each one shows a one-line description.
- **2:47–5:30** I did "Pop the kettle by hand, perfect pops in a row" and got gold with 8 in a row (+60). I lost two presses that came just after a batch burst; waiting about 3 seconds fixed that.
- **6:16–10:23** I did "Serve as many customers as you can" and got gold, 324 customers (+160). I barely touched it; a rush event carried it.
- **6:48–9:52** I explored the info cards:
  - The shop and kitchen cards had a bottom button with only a star on it, and tapping it did nothing.
  - The coin-and-spatula button opened an overview: popcorn 96, buckets 89, coins 87, with the 87 boxed in red.
  - Tapping a small machine opened a cheap upgrade (I bought one for 450). Its status line flickered between a green dot and a red minus.
  - Closing a card took me several tries. The section's own round button turned out to be what closes it.
- **10:54** I tried to start the scale challenge ("earn more a minute than your best"), but the rows had shifted and I restarted the popping challenge instead. Gold again, but no new trophy cup.
- **13:45–17:22** I started "Pack bags without giving popcorn away". The counter stayed at 0.0 and I had packed 315 of 12 bags without doing anything. I bought a scale machine (400); its card still said "scale 0" afterwards.
- **16:45** I bought the 14,000 cart upgrade and the cart got poles and lamps.
- **17:49** Holding for only 0.8 seconds finally explained the ring to me. The needle sweeps round while you hold: blue means too early, yellow is the sweet spot, red is burnt.
- **18:33–19:33** I noticed the kitchen had a red frame and most cart machines had red minus badges. I upgraded a small kitchen filler twice. The red frame and the minus badges disappeared, and the button turned into a plain star. So **star = maxed**, and a **red frame = the bottleneck**.

**2. What was fun and what wasn't**
- **Fun:**
  - The hold-to-pop timing, with smoke when it burns and a popcorn burst when a batch finishes.
  - Climbing from bronze to silver to gold in the popping challenge.
  - Watching the red frame and the minus badges vanish after the right upgrade.
- **Not fun:**
  - The customer and packing challenges won themselves, so the golds felt hollow.
  - The rewards (60 or 160 coins) were tiny next to an income of roughly 1,000 a minute.
  - Popping got repetitive once I had the rhythm.
  - Some upgrades, like the scale, showed no visible effect.

**3. What confused me**
- The star button. I tapped it several times before I learned at 19:20 that it means maxed.
- What the red minus badge and the red frame meant. I worked out the bottleneck idea only at 18:33, and that is the actual strategy.
- Closing the info cards.
- Presses being ignored right after a batch, with nothing on screen to say the kettle wasn't ready.
- The needle is thin and easy to miss.
- The rows in the challenge list shift, which made me start the wrong challenge.
- The sparkle button lights up but changes nothing I could see.
- What "giving popcorn away" looks like.
- The numbers in the overview sheet and the tags like "89 / 6".

**4. Would I keep playing?** Probably a little longer. I only understood the core loop (find the red section, upgrade its small machines) in the last two minutes, and I want to try it on purpose, plus get the two empty trophy cups. But the next cart upgrade costs 27,000, the challenge rewards are small, and the popping is repetitive. Without a clear bigger goal, I'd likely drift off within a session or two.

**5.** I used only the play commands and read only the screenshots they printed. I opened no other files or folders, didn't browse, and looked for no code or documentation.

**6. What I think the goal is:** grow the popcorn shop into a balanced production line, from cart to kitchen to counter. You do that by finding whichever section is the bottleneck and upgrading its machines. On top of that you pop the kettle by hand with good timing, and collect gold trophies in timed challenges to fill the five-cup row.
