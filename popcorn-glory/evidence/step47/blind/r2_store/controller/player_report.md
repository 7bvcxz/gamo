**Player report: a popcorn shop game, played on a phone for 20 minutes**

**1. Timeline**
- **0:00–0:50** I saw a three-floor popcorn shop: a counter with customers, a middle room with a hopper and a crane that fills cups, and a red popcorn machine with a coloured ring around it. Tapping the machine sent popcorn upstairs, the crane filled cups and customers paid. I had 48 coins after about 10 taps.
- **0:57** Bought the first machine upgrade (30 coins). It got a fancier roof and confetti flew.
- **1:05–1:32** Found the real mechanic. When I hold the machine, a needle goes round the ring. If I let go in the yellow part I get a golden burst and a gold pip under the machine. Holding about 1.7–1.8 seconds worked almost every time. When all 9 pips fill, there's a big golden shower and they reset.
- **1:39–2:07** Noticed a red outline that moves to whichever room is the slow one. Learned that each room's icon switches the upgrade card at the bottom to that room.
- **2:15** Upgraded the counter so customers came more often.
- **2:57–5:47** The newspaper gave me a challenge: "Pop the kettle by hand. Count the perfect pops in a row." The goal was 8 in a row in 3 minutes. My holds stopped working at a streak of 4 because the hopper and shelf were full. The streak didn't reset, though. I bought another counter upgrade to clear the jam and got gold at 5:47 (+60 coins).
- **6:21–6:30** The news tab turned out to be a log of "movie crowd" events: customers ×1.5 for about 75–90 seconds.
- **6:40–7:01** The locked slots next to the machine did nothing yet. I pressed the sparkle button twice and never worked out what it does.
- **7:12–8:06** Worked out the customer number on the counter card: 60 ÷ the customer timer × the star multiplier. Later (13:50) I realised the big jumps in the star multiplier were just the movie bonus.
- **9:02–9:50** Another machine upgrade unlocked the slots. They are build menus for extra gadgets (a small machine, a lever, a wheel), each with numbers but no explanation.
- **10:00–10:32** A movie crowd emptied my cup shelf completely, so I upgraded the cup room: the crane became a dispenser tube.
- **13:02** A second challenge appeared: "Serve as many customers as you can." Its targets went up after I upgraded the counter, and it stayed locked while a movie was on.
- **14:40–17:25** Replayed the streak challenge and got gold again with about 20 seconds to spare. I learned to wait until the popcorn in the machine settled before holding.
- **17:44–19:58** Started the serve challenge and bought an extra dispenser when the shop sold out. The queue grew to 6 people, then cleared, and I reached 35 served. My session ended before the challenge did.

**2. What was fun and what wasn't**
- **Fun:**
  - The hold-and-release timing is easy to learn and feels good, with a golden burst on every perfect pop.
  - Watching the red outline move from room to room as I fixed each jam made the shop feel like a real system.
  - The second gold medal with seconds left was genuinely tense.
- **Not fun:**
  - During the streak challenge I spent most of the time waiting for the machine to empty, not timing. About half my holds were silently swallowed, with no message saying "full".

**3. What confused me**
- Most stats are icons and numbers with no labels, for example "★ 600 &gt; 650" and "spinning arrow 350 &gt; 350".
- I couldn't tell what the lever, wheel and teal gadgets do.
- I never learned what the sparkle button does.
- The serve challenge's targets moved and the challenge was locked during movies, with nothing on screen saying why.
- I was never sure whether "+14 ▸ +18" on the machine is coins or popcorn, since customers paid 10–17.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Probably yes, for a while. I wanted to see the next machine and dispenser upgrades, fill the remaining trophy slots and try the gadgets. If the gadgets stay unexplained and challenges keep stalling on a full machine, I'd likely drift off.

**5. Confirmation:** I used only the play commands (look, tap, hold, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed.

**6. What I think the goal is:** Grow a popcorn shop by keeping production balanced across the three rooms. Pop by hand with good timing, find the slowest room and upgrade or add gadgets there, handle the movie rushes, and collect trophies from the newspaper challenges.
