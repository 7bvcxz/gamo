2. Fun and not fun
- Fun: The release timing feels good, especially the golden burst on a perfect. The upgrade cards say clearly when I can afford them. The machine visibly grows. The jars are a real risk-versus-speed choice. Watching coins arrive while idle was pleasant.
- Not fun: After about 5 minutes the same press-and-wiggle gesture got repetitive. The gear upgrade gave me nothing because it only replaced my stirring bonus. The customers at the top barely matter.

3. Confusion

- I didn't know what the line of customers at the top was for.
- "x1.15" on the gear card didn't say what it multiplies.

- I didn't know the back arrow led to other modes until the last minute.

4. Would I keep playing? For a few more minutes, yes, because I'd want to see the 14,000 upgrade and compare modes A and B. Past that, probably not: the core gesture never changes, and the costs climb faster than anything new appears.
