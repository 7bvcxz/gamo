2. What was fun and what wasn't
- Fun: the perfect pop, with gold popcorn and +10/+16, felt great after burning it so often. Watching the bottom card fill up and light up when I could afford the upgrade was clear and satisfying. Each upgrade visibly changes the machine and raises the payout.
- Not fun: the first minute and a half went to burning and guessing.

3. What confused me
- I couldn't see which way the needle moves or how fast. I only ever saw it after I let go, sometimes while it was springing back to 12. Because of that, a needle "at 10:30 in orange" looked like early progress, when it actually meant nearly burnt. I spent four tries holding longer when I should have been holding shorter.
- Nothing tells you that orange means "almost burnt" rather than "warming up". Orange-then-yellow going counterclockwise reads like a heat-up scale.
- I never found out what the new white segment after 12 o'clock is for.
- On a real phone I would see the needle moving while I hold, so this is probably much less confusing in normal play. With snapshot-only views it was hard.

4. Would I keep playing?
For a while, yes. The upgrade loop works: hit the yellow, buy a bigger machine, the yellow gets wider, pops pay more. I want to see what comes after the cart at 97, and what the white zone does. Once the timing is learned, though, it's the same 1.5 s hold over and over. I'd need the next upgrades to change the timing or add something new to stay past ten minutes.
