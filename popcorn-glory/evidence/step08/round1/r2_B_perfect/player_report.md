- The bucket filling up gold is a nice wordless progress meter.

- After every tap the needle sat at 12 o'clock, so it looked like a fixed marker, not something that moves.

- The smoke was unexplained.
- The growing brown ring looked like a timing cue but did nothing I could find.

4. Keep playing?
Yes. Once the hold-and-release timing clicked, the loop is clear and tactile, and the upgrade changing the timing gives me a fresh skill to learn. A player who never stumbles onto the release trick would probably quit in the first two minutes, though.
