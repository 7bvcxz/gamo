I played the full five minutes. I got the hang of the shop and the upgrades, but never worked out how to time the cooking reliably.

**1. Timeline**
- **0:00** First look: a popcorn shop with three stacked panels. Customers wait at a counter on top, a station fills cups in the middle, and a big red cart sits at the bottom with a coloured ring around it. There's a yellow upgrade button at the bottom (175 coins, +11 ▸ +14). I expected tapping the cart to make popcorn.
- **0:09–0:16** I tapped the cart and coins went up by about 12–14 each time. Popcorn flew to the middle station and a customer was served.
- **0:22** I bought the cart upgrade. The cart got bigger and a star appeared. The next upgrade cost 430.
- **0:33–0:52** A 2-second hold lit 2 of the 9 dots under the cart. A 4-second hold gave big black smoke and brown popcorn in the jar. That was my "aha": the ring is a cooking clock, with blue meaning raw, yellow meaning golden and red meaning burnt. A 2.3-second hold stopped in the red.
- **1:06–1:16** Holds of 1.8 seconds were golden and the dots rose to 4, then 6. I bought the 430 upgrade.
- **1:25–1:35** Holds of 1.9 and 1.6 seconds gave smoke and no new dots. A small machine in the middle station was also smoking.
- **1:49–1:58** Tapping that smoking machine opened an info card. It showed 24 > 28, a red −3, a bar at 36/900 and a timer at ~53, plus a locked chocolate cup. I paid 400 to upgrade it, and the bar's target jumped to 1300 and the timer to ~81.
- **2:07** A 1.7-second hold set off a popcorn explosion and the dots reset to empty, so I think I filled the meter.
- **2:16–3:11** Everything smoked after that: holds of 1.7, 1.4, 1.2 (after cooling for 5 seconds) and 0.7 seconds, and even a plain tap. When I left it alone there was no smoke, and coins kept rising by themselves.
- **3:27–3:34** The round icon at the top-left of the cart panel selected the cart and showed its stats (18>23, 700>750, 300>300). I bought the 1050 upgrade and the cart got wider.
- **3:45** A 1.5-second hold was golden again. I wondered whether the burn streak was linked to the other machine's card being open.
- **3:54–4:30** More smoke: 1.6, 1.45, 1.4 and 1.0 seconds all burnt. The counter was often empty and its panel had a red outline. Tapping the counter's icon showed 17.1 > 27.3 and 3.5 > 2.2 for 1,100 coins, which I couldn't afford.
- **4:36–5:01** I waited and saved up. Time ran out.

**2. What was fun or satisfying**
- Working out the cook-ring by myself, from a burnt batch, felt great.
- A golden pop with a shower of kernels and a new dot lit is a lovely reward. The popcorn explosion when the meter filled was the best moment.
- Upgrades feel solid: the cart visibly grows and the numbers go up.
- Selecting the counter, cart or a machine to see its own upgrade is a neat idea once you find it.

**What wasn't fun**
- After the explosion, nearly every attempt burnt. Holds a tenth of a second apart gave opposite results (1.5 golden, then 1.6, 1.45, 1.4 and 1.0 all burnt), so it felt random rather than a skill.

**3. What confused me**
- I never saw the needle move while holding. I only saw where it stopped afterwards, so I couldn't learn the timing.
- I couldn't tell whether smoke meant burnt, wasted because there was nowhere to put it, or the cart overheating.
- The red "−3" on the caramel card worried me and I never learned what it meant.
- Upgrading the caramel machine pushed its progress target from 900 to 1300 and the timer from ~53 to ~81. It felt like I'd been punished.
- The small stat numbers (700>750, 300>260, 17.1>27.3) had no labels.
- I couldn't find a way to close a selection card.
- The red outline seemed to mark the bottleneck, but I only guessed that late.

**4. Would I keep playing?**
Maybe for a little while. The shop is charming, earns money on its own, and I wanted the 1,100 counter upgrade and the locked chocolate cup. But the main action stopped making sense after a minute, and that took most of the fun out of tapping. If I could see the needle sweep while holding, and knew why things burn, I'd happily keep going.

I used only the play commands (look, tap, hold, wait, note, status) and read only the screenshots they printed.
