My three minutes are up. The game is a popcorn machine inside a ring-shaped heat gauge. It had no words at all, and I still worked it out from the screen alone.

1. Timeline
- 0:00–0:08 First look: a red popcorn machine inside a ring. The ring had a red arc on the left, a pale wedge at the lower left, a blue arc with clockwise arrows, and a smoke puff on top. Coins: 0. A bottom card showed a pot, "30" and "+10 ▸ +16". My guess: tap the machine to pop corn, and the card is an upgrade I can't afford yet.
- 0:12 I tapped the machine once and got +1. A grey needle flashed at 12 o'clock, and the pale wedge turned solid yellow. I guessed heat travels clockwise: blue, then yellow (good), then red (burnt).
- 0:34 I held for 2 s and got +5. The needle stopped at 10 o'clock in the red, so I'd overshot. That told me holding sweeps the needle and letting go scores wherever it stops. A yellow bar started filling the upgrade card.
- 0:47 and 0:56 Holds of 1.6 s and 1.7 s landed in the yellow, +10 each time, with popcorn spraying out. I saw that +10 matched the "+10" on the card.
- 1:07–1:13 At 36 coins the card lit up (red arrow, red "+16"). I bought it. The machine got bigger and fancier, with a striped awning and bunting. Next upgrade: 54 coins for +16 ▸ +26.
- 1:25–2:01 I lost the rhythm. 1.7 s now stopped at 5 o'clock (+3). 2.5 s burnt it completely: a big black smoke cloud and +2. 2.05 s still landed in the red, with brown burnt kernels (+4). 1.85 s hit the yellow/red border (+8, pale popcorn).
- 2:09–2:20 Two holds of 1.78 s were perfect: +16 each with a golden burst. Here I noticed the popcorn colour grades the pop: golden is perfect, pale is off, brown is burnt.
- 2:25 I bought the second upgrade. It turned into a wheeled popcorn cart. Next upgrade: 97 coins for +26 ▸ +40.
- 2:38–2:48 Two holds of 1.8–1.85 s gave +26 each, leaving me on 53 coins. The last hold went in at 3:00, after time ran out, and I never looked at its result.

2. What felt good, and what didn't
- Good: finding the hold-and-release mechanic with no instructions. A golden popcorn burst with a floating "+16" is a nice payoff. Each upgrade visibly changes the machine (pot, then fancy machine, then cart), which made me want to see the next one. Popcorn colour as feedback is a clever touch.
- Not good: after the first upgrade my learned timing stopped working, and I got three bad pops in a row (+3, +2, +4). That felt like being punished for upgrading. The burnt smoke cloud stings, though in a fair way.

3. What confused me
- My first tap gave only +1, and at first I couldn't tell whether tapping or holding was the right input.
- I couldn't tell whether the needle speed really changed after the upgrade or whether my timing was just inconsistent. Nothing on screen says the speed changed.
- The needle is hidden while you hold (I only saw where it stopped), so I was aiming blind and learning only from results.
- I never pressed the back, sound or restart buttons, so I don't know what they do.

4. Would I keep playing? Yes, for a while. I want to see what comes after the cart, and landing a golden pop is satisfying. Long term, though, it's one action repeated, so it needs new twists (a moving yellow zone, combos, new machines) to hold me.
